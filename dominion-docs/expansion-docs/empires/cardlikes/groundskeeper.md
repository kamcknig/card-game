Groundskeeper is an Action card from Empires. It's a cantrip that gains  VP tokens when you gain a Victory card.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

This turn, when you gain a Victory card, +1VP.

## Unofficial FAQ (2022)

- This can trigger multiple times in a turn, for cards gained different ways. For example you could play Groundskeeper, then play Engineer gaining an Estate and taking +1 VP, then in your Buy phase buy a Duchy taking another +1 VP.
- Multiple Groundskeepers are cumulative.

## Other rules clarifications

- In 2022, Groundskeeper got errata that changed it from a "while this is in play" ability to a "this turn" ability. This means that now if you use Crown to play a Groundskeeper twice, then each Victory card you gain that turn will come with 2 VP.
