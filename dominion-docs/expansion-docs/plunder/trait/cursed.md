Cursed is a Trait from Plunder. When you gain a card with this Trait, you also get two other cards: a Loot card and a
Curse.

## Official FAQ

When you gain a card from the Cursed pile, you also gain a Loot and a Curse.
If there are no Curses left, you still gain a Loot.

## Other rules clarifications

You gain the Cursed card first, then the Loot, and finally the Curse.

## card text

When you gain a Cursed card, gain a Loot and a Curse.
