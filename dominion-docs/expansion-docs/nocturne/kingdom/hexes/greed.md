Fear is a Hex from Nocturne. It is a handsize attack that forces you to discard an Action or Treasure from your hand (if you have at least 5 cards in hand).

## Official FAQ

- You discard an Action or Treasure if you have either, and only reveal your
  hand if you have no Actions and no Treasures.

## card text

If you have at least 5 cards in hand, discard an Action or Treasure (or reveal you can't).
