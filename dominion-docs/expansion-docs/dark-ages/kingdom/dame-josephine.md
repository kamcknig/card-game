Dame Josephine is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights,
she is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. Unlike the
other Knights, she is also a Victory card worth 2 VP.

## Official FAQ

Full FAQ on the Knights page. The points specific to Dame Josephine are
reproduced below.

Dame Josephine is also a Victory card, worth 2VP at the end of the game. The
Knights pile is not a Victory pile though, and does not get a counter for Trade
Route (from Prosperity) even if Dame Josephine starts on top.

## card text

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them
costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this,
trash this.

2VP
