Tomb is a Landmark from Empires. It gives players +1VP whenever they trash a card.

## card text

When you trash a card, +1VP.

## Official FAQ

- This works even when it is not your turn, such as when you trash a card to Swindler (from Dominion: Intrigue), and works when told to trash a card that is not yours, such as with Salt the Earth.

## Other rules clarifications

- You get +1VP for each card you trash, even if you trash multiple cards at the same time.
