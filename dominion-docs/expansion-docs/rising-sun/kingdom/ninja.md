Ninja is an Action-Attack-Shadow card from Rising Sun. It is a discard attack that you can play from your deck without
having to wait to draw it first.

## Official FAQ

- See the Shadows section.
- When you play Ninja, you draw a card, and each other player discards down to 3 cards in hand.

## card text

+1 Card

Each other player discards down to 3 cards in hand.

You can play this from your deck as if in your hand.
