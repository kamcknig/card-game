Progress is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it is activated, cards you gain are automatically top-decked for the rest
of the game. This can be great when buying engine pieces, allowing you to accelerate your deck-building, but becomes a
nuisance when it's time to start greening.

## Official FAQ

- This is not optional; all gained cards go onto your deck.
- This includes cards gained when it's not your turn.

## Other rules clarifications

- This also applies to cards that would normally be gained to unusual locations, such as a Den of Sin or anything gained
  with Blockade.
- However, the stop-moving rule can in some cases prevent a card from being placed on your deck. For instance, you may
  use a Sleigh to move a gained card into your hand; if you do, Progress will no longer be able to move it onto your
  deck.
- You may have to read card texts carefully to determine whether something is gained directly to an unusual location (in
  which case Progress overrules it) or is moved after being gained (so the stop-moving rule can apply). For instance,
  although Ghost Town and Villa may appear similar in their gaining behavior, Ghost Town is gained to the hand (and must
  be moved to the deck by Progress), whereas Villa moves itself to the hand after being gained (which can overrule
  Progress).

## card text

When you gain a card, put it onto your deck.
