The Flag is an Artifact from Renaissance. It increases the size of your next hand when drawing, which is usually advantageous, but is negated when an opponent plays a discard attack afterwards. There is one copy of it, and players can take it from each other when gaining or trashing a Flag Bearer.

see [flag bearer](../kingdom/flag-bearer.md) for faq

## card text

When drawing your hand, +1 Card.
