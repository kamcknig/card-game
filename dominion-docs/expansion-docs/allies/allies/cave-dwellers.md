Cave Dwellers is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to use Favor tokens as a
source of sifting.

## Official FAQ

- At the start of your turn, you may spend a Favor; if you do, you discard a
  card and then draw a card. Then you can spend another Favor to discard another
  card and draw another card, and so on, until you stop spending Favors.
- You draw a card even if you failed to discard one.

## card text

At the start of your turn, you may spend a Favor, to discard a card then draw a
card. Repeat as desired.
