Practice is an Event from Rising Sun. It allows you to throne an Action during the Buy phase.

## Official FAQ

If you use this on a Duration card, you may wish to tilt the card to remind you that you played it twice.

## Other rules clarifications

You can't play Treasure cards in your Buy phase after buying something, so if you draw any Treasure cards as a result of
the Action you play with Practice, you will ordinarily not be able to play them; if you draw any Action cards, you can
play them by buying Practice again.

## card text

You may play an Action card from your hand twice.
