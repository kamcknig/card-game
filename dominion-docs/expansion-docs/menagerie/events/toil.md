Toil is an Event from Menagerie. It allows the buyer to play an Action card from
their hand in the Buy phase.

## Official FAQ

- Playing an Action card this way does not use up an Action.

## Other rules clarifications

- You can't play Treasure cards in your Buy phase after buying something, so
  if you draw any Treasure cards as a result of the Action you play with Toil,
  you will ordinarily not be able to play them; if you draw any Action cards,
  you can play them by buying Toil again.

## card text

+1 Buy

You may play an Action card from your hand.
