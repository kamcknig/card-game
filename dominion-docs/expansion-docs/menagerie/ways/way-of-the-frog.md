Way of the Frog is a Way from Menagerie. It allows you to non-terminally topdeck
an Action card for your next turn, instead of receiving its effects this turn.

## Official FAQ

- Way of the Frog has no official FAQ.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you Throne Room a Duration card (like Wharf), and play it as Way of the
  Frog on the second play, you will get +1 Action, but since Wharf isn't getting
  discarded from play this turn, it will not topdeck itself on your next turn.

## card text

+1 Action

When you discard this from play this turn, put it onto your deck.
