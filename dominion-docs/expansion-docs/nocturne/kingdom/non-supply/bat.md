Bat is a Night card from Nocturne. A Bat is what a Vampire turns into when you play it, and so one function of Bat (from a game design standpoint) is to prevent you from playing your Vampire too often. Bat is a trasher, and the only way to get your Vampire back is by trashing with Bat.

## Official FAQ

- The Vampire is put into your discard pile.
- If there are no Vampires in their pile, you cannot exchange Bat for one,
  but can still trash cards.

## card text

Bat from Shuffle iT

Trash up to 2 cards from your hand. If you trashed at least one, exchange this for a Vampire.

(This is not in the Supply.)
