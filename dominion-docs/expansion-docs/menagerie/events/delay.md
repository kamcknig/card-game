Delay is an Event from Menagerie. It sets aside an Action to be played
automatically at the start of your next turn.

## Official FAQ

- Once you set aside the Action card, you have to play it at the start of
  your next turn.
- If you do multiple things at the start of your turn, you can order them.
- Playing the Action card at the start of your next turn does not use up an
  Action.

## card text

You may set aside an Action card from your hand. At the start of your next turn,
play it.
