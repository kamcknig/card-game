Rocks is a Treasure card from Empires. It's part of a split pile, with 5 copies of Rocks sitting under 5 copies of Catapult. Rocks is designed to be the optimal trashing target for Catapult: as a Treasure costing $4, it allows Catapult to function as both a curser and a handsize attack; and when you gain or trash it you get an extra Silver, which can also serve as ammunition for your Catapults.

## card text

$1

When you gain or trash this: If it's your Buy phase, gain a Silver onto your deck, otherwise gain a Silver to your hand.

## Official FAQ

- If it is another player's turn, then it is not your Buy phase, so the Silver goes to your hand.

## Other rules clarifications

- The Silver is gained directly to your deck or hand; it does not visit your discard pile.
