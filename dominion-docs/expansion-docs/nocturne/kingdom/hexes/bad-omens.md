Bad Omens is a Hex from Nocturne. It is a deck order attack, forcing you to discard your deck and topdeck two Coppers.

## Official FAQ

- Normally you will end up with a deck consisting of two Coppers, and a
  discard pile with the rest of your cards.
- Sometimes you will only have one or no Coppers; in those cases reveal your
  deck to demonstrate this.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you have only one Copper, put it on your deck.
- This doesn't count as discarding, so it will not activate Tunnel, Faithful
  Hound, or Village Green.

## card text

Put your deck into your discard pile. Look through it and put 2 Coppers from it onto your deck (or reveal you can't).
