Deliver is an Event from Plunder. It allows you to put gained cards directly in your hand at the end of your turn, so
you'll have them for your next turn.

## Official FAQ

Buying this more than once doesn't do anything extra.
The set aside cards go into your hand after drawing your usual 5 cards.

## card text

+1 Buy

This turn, each time you gain a card, set it aside, and put it into your hand at end of turn.
