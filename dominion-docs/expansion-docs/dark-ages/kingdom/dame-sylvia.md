Dame Sylvia is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights,
she is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. She is also a
terminal Silver.

## card text

+$2

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them
costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this,
trash this.
