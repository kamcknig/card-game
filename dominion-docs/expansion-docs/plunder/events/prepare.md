Prepare is an Event from Plunder. If you have cards remaining in your hand in your Buy phase, Prepare will allow you to
save them all for next turn and play them then without using up any Action plays.

## Official FAQ

Once you've set the cards aside, playing all of those Actions and Treasures next turn is mandatory.

## Other rules clarifications

Between playing each of the set-aside cards, you cannot play any cards from your hand, unless a card specifically tells
you so (for example, Throne Room).
Playing all the set-aside cards is a single start-of-turn effect. Between playing each of those cards, you cannot
resolve any other start-of-turn effects (for example, from Durations played last turn).

## card text

Set aside your hand face up. At the start of your next turn, play those Actions and Treasures in any order, then discard
the rest.
