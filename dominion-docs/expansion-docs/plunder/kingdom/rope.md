Rope is a Treasure-Duration card from Plunder. It provides +Buy when you play it, and is a trasher on the following
turn.

## Official FAQ

When you play this, you get +$1 and +1 Buy, and at the start of your next turn, you first draw a card, then may trash
a card from your hand.

## card text

$1

+1 Buy

At the start of your next turn, +1 Card and you may trash a card from your hand.
