Fortune Hunter is an Action card from Plunder. It is a terminal Action that
allows you to search the top few cards of your deck for a Treasure, and if you
find one, play it during your Action phase..

## Official FAQ

Completely resolve playing the Treasure before putting the other cards back on
top; for example if the Treasure is a Figurine, the two cards you draw won't be
the other ones you looked at with Fortune Hunter.

## card text

+$2

Look at the top 3 cards of your deck. You may play a Treasure from them. Put the
rest back in any order.
