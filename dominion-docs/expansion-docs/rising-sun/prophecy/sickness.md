Sickness is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it's activated, it inflicts harsh discard or cursing attacks every turn
until the Curses are depleted.

## Official FAQ

- You can choose to gain a Curse even if the Curse pile is empty, or to discard cards even if you have fewer than 3
  cards in hand (in which case, discard as many as you can).

## card text

At the start of your turn, choose one: Gain a Curse onto your deck; or discard 3 cards.
