Doubloons is a Treasure-Loot card from Plunder. Like most Loot cards, it provides +$3$3; and this one comes with a free
Gold as well. Since Doubloons' on-play ability is the same as that of Gold, gaining Doubloons is essentially equivalent
to just gaining two Golds at once.

## Official FAQ
