Sibyl is an Action-Augur card from Allies. It is a source of non-terminal draw
that allows you to organize your deck a bit by putting cards of your choice at
the top and bottom. It has similarities to Secret Passage.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Augurs: Herb Gatherer,
Acolyte, and Sorceress.

## Official FAQ

- If after drawing your deck has no cards in it, the first card you put back
  will become the top card of it.

## card text

+4 Cards

+1 Action

Put a card from your hand on top of your deck, and another on the bottom.
