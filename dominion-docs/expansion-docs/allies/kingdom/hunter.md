Hunter is an Action card from Allies. It acts as a source of non-terminal draw
or a sifter, depending on the types of the cards on top of your deck.

## Official FAQ

- From the three cards, choose an Action, then a Treasure, then a Victory card.
- Cards with multiple types can be chosen for any matching type. For example,
  if the revealed cards were Stronghold, Copper, Silver, you would have to take
  Stronghold as the Action, would choose between Silver and Copper as the
  Treasure, and would get no Victory card. Then you would discard the unchosen
  Treasure.

## card text

+1 Action

Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. From those cards, put an Action, a
Treasure, and a Victory card into your hand. Discard the rest.
