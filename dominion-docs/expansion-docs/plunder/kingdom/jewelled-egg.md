Jewelled Egg is a Treasure card from Plunder. It is a weak source of +Buy, but
if you can trash it you gain a much more valuable Loot card.

## Official FAQ

The player trashing Jewelled Egg gets the Loot, regardless of which player
played the card that caused them to trash it.

## card text

$1

+1 Buy

When you trash this, gain a Loot.
