Swap is an Action from Allies. It is a cantrip that allows you to transform any
Action card in your hand into a different Action card.

## Official FAQ

- First you get +1 Card and +1 Action. Then you may return an Action card
  from your hand to its pile; this is optional. If you do, then gain an Action
  card from the Supply costing up to $5, and put it into your hand.
- The card you gain can't have the same name as the one you returned.
- Returning the card isn't trashing it, and won't trigger "when you trash
  this" abilities; gaining the card is gaining it, and will trigger "when you
  gain this" abilities.

## Other rules clarifications

- A non-Supply card (like Horse or cards gained with Ferryman) can be
  returned to its pile. However, a card with no pile (like Necropolis, or
  anything from the Black Market deck) can't be returned anywhere.
- A card from a split pile can be returned even if a different card is
  currently on top of the pile. The returned card is returned to the top of the
  pile even if other copies of the card are further down.
- You can't gain a card that's not on top of its pile, which means you can't
  return a card and gain a card from the same pile. So if Sorceress is on top of
  the Augurs pile, and you return a Herb Gatherer, you can't gain the Sorceress
  because it's no longer on top.
- If you Possess another player and make them Swap an Action card, their
  Action returns to its pile, and then you gain a differently named Action card
  to your discard pile.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

You may return an Action from your hand to its pile, to gain to your hand a
different Action costing up to $5.
