Circle of Witches is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to spend Favor tokens to
use Liaison cards as cursers.

## Official FAQ

- After you completely resolve playing a Liaison, you may spend 3 Favors to
  have each other player gain a Curse. This can include Favors you just got from
  playing that Liaison.
- This is not playing an Attack card and cannot be blocked with Moat.

## card text

After playing a Liaison, you may spend 3 Favors to have each other player gain a
Curse.
