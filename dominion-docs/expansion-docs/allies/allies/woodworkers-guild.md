Woodworkers' Guild is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to use Favors to trash
Action cards and replace them with other Action cards.

## Official FAQ

- This can gain an Action of any cost, including Actions with DD or PP in the
  cost. You only gain an Action if you trashed one.

## card text

At the start of your Buy phase, you may spend a Favor to trash an Action card
from your hand. If you did, gain an Action card.
