Werewolf is an Action-Night-Attack-Doom card from Nocturne. It's a terminal draw card during the day (i.e., your Action phase), but can also be played during the Night phase to inflict Hexes on your opponents.

## Official FAQ

- Werewolf can be played in either your Action phase or Night phase.
- If played in your Action phase, you draw three cards; if played at Night,
  each other player receives the next Hex.

## Other rules clarifications

- As always, a card with multiple types retains those types in all contexts.
  For instance, when you play Werewolf in the Action phase (so it doesn't attack), it's still an Attack card and activates other players' Diplomats and so on. And when you play it in the Night phase, it's still an Action card, so you can call Royal Carriage to repeat the Hexing, for example.
- If you have abilities to resolve at the start of your turn, it is
  considered part of your Action phase, and you cannot end it. This means if you play Werewolf at the start of your turn (with e.g, Delay), you cannot skip to your Night phase and give out a Hex.
- If you play it during any phase that isn't your own Night phase (such as
  with Scepter, or March), including during another player's Night Phase, you get +3 Cards.

## card text

If it's your Night phase, each other player receives the next Hex. Otherwise, +3 Cards.
