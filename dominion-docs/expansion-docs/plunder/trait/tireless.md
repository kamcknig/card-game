Tireless is a Trait from Plunder. Cards with this trait top-deck themselves when you discard them from play, instead of
staying in your discard pile.

## Official FAQ

This is mandatory.
You draw your next hand before putting the card onto your deck.

## Other rules clarifications

If a Tireless card never gets discarded from play (e.g. it's Quartermaster, Crew, or a one-shot), then Tireless will
have no effect.
If something else moves a Tireless card when it's discarded from play (e.g. Scheme), you won't set it aside.
If a Tireless card discards itself from play at a weird time (e.g. Highwayman), you'll still set it aside and put it
onto your deck at the end of the turn.
If a split pile is Tireless, you can put the cards onto your deck in any order.
If you have an ability that gives you +Cards at the end of your turn (from e.g. Way of the Squirrel or Farrier), you can
top-deck your Tireless card before or after taking the +Cards. If you top-deck first, you will draw the Tireless card
immediately and it will be in your hand during your opponent's turn.
Note that some things draw extra cards when you draw your Clean-up hand (e.g. Flag or Expedition), which will happen
before you topdeck Tireless cards.

## card text

When you discard a Tireless card from play, set it aside, and put it onto your deck at end of turn.
