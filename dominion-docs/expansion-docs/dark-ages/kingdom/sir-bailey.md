Sir Bailey is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights, he
is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. He is also a
cantrip.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them
costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this,
trash this.
