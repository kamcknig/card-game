Pickaxe is a Treasure card from Plunder. You can use it as a simple trasher for
your junk cards; or if you choose to trash more valuable cards, you'll be
compensated with Loot.

## Official FAQ

Trashing is mandatory, if you have any cards left in hand. Remember that you
have to reveal the gained Loot.

## card text

$1

Trash a card from your hand. If it costs $3 or more, gain a Loot to your hand.
