Rustic Village is an Action-Omen card from Rising Sun. It is a village that provides a small amount of sifting. As an
Omen, it also brings Prophecies closer to taking effect.

## Official FAQ

- First the +1Sun happens, which may trigger a Prophecy; then you get +1 Card, +2 Actions, and may discard 2 cards (
  including the one just drawn) for another +1 Card.

## card text

+1Sun

+1 Card

+2 Actions

You may discard 2 cards for +1 Card.
