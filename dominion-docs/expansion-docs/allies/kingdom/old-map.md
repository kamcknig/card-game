Old Map is an Action-Odyssey card from Allies. It is a cantrip that provides
limited handsize-neutral sifting.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Odysseys: Voyage,
Sunken Treasure, and Distant Shore; and it allows rotating the pile.

## Official FAQ

- Everything happens in the order listed: first you get +1 Card and +1 Action;
  then you discard a card; then you draw a card; then you choose whether or not
  to rotate the Odysseys.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

Discard a card. +1 Card.

You may rotate the Odysseys.
