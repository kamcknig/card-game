Rice Broker is an Action card from Rising Sun. It is a trasher that gives a large amount of non-terminal draw if you
trash an Action card, and a smaller amount if you trash a Treasure.

## Official FAQ

If you trash a card that's both a Treasure and an Action, you get +2 Cards and then +5 Cards. If you trash a card with
neither type, such as Province, you don't draw any cards.

## card text

+1 Action

Trash a card from your hand. If it's a Treasure, +2 Cards. If it's an Action, +5 Cards.
