Desert Guides is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to spend Favor tokens to
discard and replace your hand at the beginning of your turn, like the Reserve
card Guide.

## Official FAQ

- After discarding your hand and drawing 5 cards, you may spend another Favor
  to do it again, repeatedly.

- ## Other rules clarifications

Unlike Guide, once you decline to spend more Favors for Desert Guides, you can't
come back to it later after performing other start-of-turn effects.

## card text

At the start of your turn, you may spend a Favor to discard your hand and draw 5
cards. Repeat as desired. 2
