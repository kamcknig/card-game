Skulk is an Action-Attack-Doom card from Nocturne. It's a +Buy card, and hands out the next Hex to each other players. It also comes with a Gold when you gain it.

## Official FAQ

- You gain the Gold whether you gained Skulk due to buying it, or gained it
  some other way.

## card text

+1 Buy

Each other player receives the next Hex.

When you gain this, gain a Gold.
