Carpenter is an Action card from Allies. It starts out as a non-terminal gainer,
but becomes a terminal remodeler later in the game.

## Official FAQ

- First see if there are any empty Supply piles.
- If there are none, you get +1 Action and gain a card costing up to $4; if
  there are one or more empty piles, instead you trash a card from your hand and
  gain a card costing up to $2 more than the card you trashed.

## card text

If no Supply piles are empty, +1 Action and gain a card costing up to $4.

Otherwise, trash a card from your hand and gain a card costing up to $2 more
than it.
