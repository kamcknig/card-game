Windfall is an Event from Empires. It rewards you with three Golds for drawing your deck, and thus gives you incentive to build a slick engine first and then let this Event provide the payload once the engine is complete.

## card text

If your deck and discard pile are empty, gain 3 Golds.

## Official FAQ

- If there are fewer than 3 Golds in the pile, just gain the remaining Golds.
