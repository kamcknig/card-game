Pooka is an Action card from Nocturne. It's a terminal draw card that requires you to trash a Treasure other than its Heirloom, Cursed Gold.

## Official FAQ

- In games using Pooka, replace one of your starting Coppers with a Cursed Gold.

## card text

You may trash a Treasure other than Cursed Gold from your hand, for +4 Cards.

Heirloom: Cursed Gold
