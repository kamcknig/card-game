Seer is an Action card from Renaissance. It is a cantrip that draws the cards of middling $ from the top 3 cards of the player's deck.

## Official FAQ

- Cards with P (from Alchemy) or D (from Empires) in their cost do not cost
  from \$2 to $4.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Put the ones costing from $2 to $4 into your hand. Put the rest back in any order.
