Scrounge is an Event from Plunder. It can be used as a trasher, or as a way to effectively buy $5 cards for $3 if you're
willing to gain an Estate from the trash.

## Official FAQ

You may either trash a card from your hand, or may gain an Estate from the trash.
If you gained an Estate, you then also gain a card costing up to $5 from the Supply.

## Other rules clarifications

You can choose to gain an Estate even if there are no Estates in the trash. If this is the case, then you don't gain any
other cards either, and you just paid $3 to do nothing. Similarly, you can also choose to trash a card from your hand
if there are no cards in you hand, in which case also nothing happens.

## card text

Choose one: Trash a card from your hand; or gain an Estate from the trash, and if you did, gain a card costing up to $5.
