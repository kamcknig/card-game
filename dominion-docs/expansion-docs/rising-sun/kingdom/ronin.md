Ronin is an Action-Shadow card from Rising Sun. It is a terminal draw-to-X card, bringing you to a handsize of 7; as a
Shadow card, it can be played from your deck when it is not in your hand.

## Official FAQ

- See the Shadows section.
- When you play this, you draw cards one at a time until you have 7 cards in hand, or can't draw any more; if you
  already had 7 or more cards in hand, you don't draw any.

## card text

Draw until you have 7 cards in hand.

You can play this from your deck as if in your hand.
