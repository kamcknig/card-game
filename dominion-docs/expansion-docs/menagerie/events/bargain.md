Bargain is an Event from Menagerie. It gains a card costing up to $5$5 for
only $4$4, but with the penalty of giving each other player a Horse.

## Official FAQ

- The other players gain their Horses in turn order.
- They cannot decline to gain one.

## card text

Gain a non-Victory card costing up to $5$5. Each other player gains a Horse.
