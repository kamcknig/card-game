Zombie Spy is an Action-Zombie card from Nocturne. It's a cantrip sifter that allows you to discard the top card from your deck if you don't want it. There is only one copy of Zombie Spy, and it is added to the trash when Necromancer is in the game.

## Official FAQ

- You draw a card before looking at the top card.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

Look at the top card of your deck. Discard it or put it back.
