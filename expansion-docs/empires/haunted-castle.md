Haunted Castle is a Victory-Castle card from Empires. It is the fourth of eight different Castles that make up the Castles split pile and comes with a free Gold when you gain it, and also has an on-gain handsize attack effect (although it does not have the the Attack type).

## card text

2VP

When you gain this during your turn, gain a Gold, and each other player with 5 or more cards in hand puts 2 of them onto their deck.

## Official FAQ

- Haunted Castle works whether you buy it or gain it some other way, provided that it is your turn.

## Other rules clarifications

- Unlike Ghost Ship, if they have more than 5 cards in hand, they only put 2 of them back.
- If gaining this activates when-gain abilities for another player (such as Road Network or Black Cat), you do Haunted Castle's ability first (assuming you gained it on your own turn).
- Just like Ill-Gotten Gains and Raid, Haunted Castle is not an Attack, so it cannot be responded to with Horse Traders, and it cannot be blocked with Moat.
