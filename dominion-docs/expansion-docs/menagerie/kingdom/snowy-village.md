Snowy Village is an Action card from Menagerie. It is a village that gives a
large number of +Actions, but at the cost of preventing you from getting any
more +Actions for the rest of the turn.

## Official FAQ

- Any extra Actions you already had before playing this do not go away; for
  example, if you play Village and then Snowy Village, you can play 5 more
  Actions after that.
- Any further +Actions you would get that turn, you do not, including from
  playing another Snowy Village or converting Villagers (from Renaissance).
- This does not stop you from playing more cards via cards that tell you to
  play cards, such as Throne Room.

## Other rules clarifications

- You can convert Villagers between drawing the card and reaching the
  sentence that prevents further +Actions.
- If you have played a Champion, you'll get +1 Action from it when you play
  Snowy Village, and then you won't get any +Actions from Champion for the rest
  of the turn. If Great Leader is in effect, Snowy Village similarly overrules
  the free +Actions it grants (including the one that it would try to give you
  after playing Snowy Village).
- If you play this with Conclave, Snowy Village will prevent you from getting
  +1 Action from Conclave.

## card text

+1 Card

+4 Actions

+1 Buy

Ignore any further +Actions you get this turn.
