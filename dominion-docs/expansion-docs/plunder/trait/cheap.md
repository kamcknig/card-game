Cheap is a Trait from Plunder. A card with this trait has its cost reduced by $1 for the entire game.

## Official FAQ

This lowers the cost of a pile for the entire game (including when scoring).
Costs can't go below $0.
This doesn't reduce non-$$ costs like PP and DD; for example this does nothing on the Engineer pile (from Empires).
This does not apply during setup; it can't for example cause a $4 to be used as Young Witch's Bane (from Cornucopia).

## card text

Cheap cards cost $1 less.
