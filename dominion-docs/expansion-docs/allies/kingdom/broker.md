Broker is an Action-Liaison card from Allies. It is a trash-for-benefit card,
similar to Apprentice and Salvager, which gives you a choice of terminal draw,
terminal $$, a village effect, or Favors in exchange for the card you trash.

## Official FAQ

- For example, if you trash an Estate, which costs $2$2, you could choose to
  get +2 Cards, or +2 Actions, or +$2$2, or +2 Favors.
- If you trash a card with DD or PP in the cost (from other expansions), you
  get nothing for those symbols.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand and choose one:
+1 Card per $1 it costs;
or +1 Action per $1 it costs;
or +$1 per $1 it costs;
or +1 Favor per 1$1 it costs.
