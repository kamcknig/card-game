Zombie Mason is an Action-Zombie card from Nocturne. It's a Remodel variant that trashes the top card of your deck, and optionally gains a card costing up to $1 more. There is only one copy of Zombie Mason, and it is added to the trash when Necromancer is in the game.

## Official FAQ

- Gaining a card is optional.
- You can gain a card costing $1 more than the trashed card, or any amount
  less; for example you can gain a copy of the trashed card.

## card text

Trash the top card of your deck. You may gain a card costing up to $1 more than it.
