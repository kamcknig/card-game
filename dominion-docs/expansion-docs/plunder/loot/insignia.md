Insignia is a Treasure-Loot card from Plunder. Like most Loot cards, it provides +$3; and Insignia also allows you to
topdeck cards when you gain them.

## Official FAQ

If you gain multiple cards, this applies to each of them - you can put any or all of them on top of your deck.

## card text

$3

This turn, when you gain a card, you may put it onto your deck.
