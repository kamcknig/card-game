Blessed Village is an Action-Fate card from Nocturne. It's a vanilla village when played, but gives you a Boon when gained, to receive either now or later.

## Official FAQ

- You see the Boon before deciding to resolve it immediately or at the start
 of your next turn
- If you save it for next turn, it sits in front of you until then (or until
  the end of that turn if it says to keep it out until Clean-up).

## Other rules clarifications

- If you gain this onto your deck (with e.g. Armory) and receive The Sea's
  Gift, cards like Gatekeeper will lose track of Blessed Village.
- When you are looking at cards from the top of your deck (with e.g. The
  Sun's Gift), that counts as moving them. So if you gain this onto your deck and then receive The Sun's Gift, that will cause Gatekeeper to lose track (even if you leave Blessed Village on top of your deck).
- If you gain a Blessed Village, discard a Blessed Village from Exile, and
  receive The Moon's Gift, and topdeck the Blessed Village you just discarded, then Changeling and Gatekeeper can still move the Blessed Village you just gained.

## card text

+1 Card

+2 Actions

When you gain this, take a Boon. Receive it now or at the start of your next turn.
