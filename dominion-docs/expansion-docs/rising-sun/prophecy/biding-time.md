Biding Time is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it is activated, players no longer discard their hands during the
Clean-up phase, but instead save their remaining cards and put them in their hand for the next turn. This can have a
substantial impact on gameplay; for example, it means that dead cards such as Victory and Curse cards no longer slow
your cycling, since after you draw them the first time they can just in effect remain in your hand indefinitely.

## Official FAQ

Instead of discarding unplayed cards in Clean-up, you set them aside, and put them back into your hand at the start of
your next turn.

## card text

At the start of your Clean-up, set aside your hand face down. At the start of your next turn, put those cards into your
hand.
