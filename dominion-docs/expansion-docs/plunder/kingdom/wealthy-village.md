Wealthy Village is an Action card from Plunder. When you play it it's a simple village, but if you can line up a variety
of different Treasure cards in play when you gain it, you can get free Loot.

## Official FAQ

The 3 differently named Treasures can include Duration Treasures you played on a previous turn, and Loots themselves.

## card text

+1 Card

+2 Actions

When you gain this, if you have at least 3 differently named Treasures in play, gain a Loot.
