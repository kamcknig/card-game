Conjurer is an Action-Duration-Wizard card from Allies. It is a gainer that puts
itself back into your hand on the turn after you play it, guaranteeing that you
can continue to use it as many turns in a row as you want.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Wizards: Student,
Sorcerer, and Lich.

## Official FAQ

- This will keep returning to your hand each turn as long as you keep
  playing it.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you play this with a Command variant such as Band of Misfits, the
  stop-moving rule means that Conjurer will fail to put itself in your hand next
  turn. Since that means Conjurer won't be doing anything next turn, the Band of
  Misfits won't stay in play and will be discarded during Clean-up of the
  current turn.[1]
- When Conjurer returns itself to your hand, any Throne Rooms that were
  staying in play will lose track of it. This means that if you play that
  Conjurer again, that Throne Room will not stay in play for a 3rd turn.

## card text

Gain a card costing up to $4$4.

At the start of your next turn, put this into your hand.
