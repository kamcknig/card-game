Night Watchman is a Night card from Nocturne. It's a sifting deck inspector that is gained to your hand for immediate use.

## Official FAQ

- Since Night is after the Buy phase, normally you can play this the turn
  you buy it.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you gain this onto your deck (with e.g. Armory), you didn't gain it to
  your discard pile, so Night Watchman's ability doesn't trigger and it stays on your deck.
- If you happen to put a card on top of your deck in the middle of resolving
  Night Watchman—for instance, by discarding a Tunnel, gaining a Gold, and top-decking it due to Progress—then when you return any remaining cards to your deck they will go on top of the card you just put there.

## card text

Look at the top 5 cards of your deck, discard any number, and put the rest back in any order.

This is gained to your hand (instead of your discard pile).
