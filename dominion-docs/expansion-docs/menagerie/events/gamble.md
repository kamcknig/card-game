Gamble is an Event from Menagerie. It may play the top card of your deck if it's
a Treasure or an Action. You are gambling $2$2 for the possibility of playing a
more valuable card. Alternatively, you may already know what card is on top of
your deck, making this a sure bet.

## Official FAQ

- Playing the card is optional and does not use up an Action.

## Other rules clarifications

- If Gamble plays a card for you that draws additional Action or Treasure
  cards into your hand, you can't play the cards it draws.

## card text

+1 Buy

Discard the top card of your deck. If it's an Action or Treasure, you may play
it.
