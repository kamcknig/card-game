Baths is a Landmark from Empires. It holds a finite number of VP tokens, which players take from when they end a turn without having gained any cards.

## card text

When you end your turn without having gained a card, take 2VP from here.

Setup: Put 6VP here per player.

## Official FAQ

- Any way you gain a card will stop you from getting VP from this that turn.

## Other Rules clarifications

- On a Possession turn, the Possessed player is the one to take 2VP from this, because they cannot gain any cards on that turn.
- If a player ends the game on a turn without gaining any cards (such as by playing a Witch and emptying the Curse pile), they will still get the 2VP from this, as the end-of-game check happens after the turn is ended.
- Exchanging a card does not count as gaining a card. As an example, if your entire turn is playing a Bat, trashing two cards, and exchanging for a Vampire, you will get the VP from Baths.
