Way of the Seal is a Way from Menagerie. It allows you to top-deck any gained
cards, similar to Royal Seal.

## Official FAQ

- This applies to all gained cards, whether bought or otherwise gained.
- The card first is gained to wherever it normally would be, then you can
  move it onto your deck. If it moves somewhere else in-between (for example,
  trashing it using Watchtower from Prosperity), you cannot move it.

## card text

+$1

This turn, when you gain a card, you may put it onto your deck.
