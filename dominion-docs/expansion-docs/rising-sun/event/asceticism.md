Asceticism is an Event from Rising Sun. It allows you to trash from your hand by paying $2 plus an amount of $ equal to
the number of cards you want to trash. Its method of use is similar to the Overpay mechanic from Guilds, though its text
doesn't use that term.

## Official FAQ

- For example you could pay an additional $3 — so $5 total — and trash 3 cards from your hand.

## card text

Pay any amount of $ to trash that many cards from your hand.
