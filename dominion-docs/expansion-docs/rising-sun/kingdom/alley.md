Alley is an Action-Shadow card from Rising Sun. It is a cantrip sifter that you can play from your deck whenever you
need it, instead of waiting for it to be in your hand. Once it's in your hand, it does nothing but reduce your handsize,
which is not usually beneficial; so it's best to make sure to play it before you end up drawing it.

## Official FAQ

- See the Shadows section.
- When you play Alley, you draw a card, get +1 Action, and discard a card.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

Discard a card.

You can play this from your deck as if in your hand.
