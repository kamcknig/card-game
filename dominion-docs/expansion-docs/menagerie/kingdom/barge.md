Barge is an Action-Duration card from Menagerie. It can provide either terminal
draw or duration draw.

## Official FAQ

- When playing Barge, choose whether to take +3 Cards and +1 Buy immediately,
  or at the start of your next turn.
- If you choose "immediately," Barge will be discarded in the same turn's
  Clean-up; if you choose "next turn," Barge will be discarded that turn.
- If you play a Barge multiple times, such as with Mastermind, you choose
  each time whether to take +3 Cards and +1 Buy immediately or next turn, and
  the Barge only stays in play until next turn if at least one of the plays was
  for next turn (in which case the Mastermind also stays in play).

## card text

Either now or at the start of your next turn, +3 Cards and +1 Buy.
