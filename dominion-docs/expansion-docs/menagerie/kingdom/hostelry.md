Hostelry is an Action card from Menagerie. It is a village that lets you discard
Treasures for Horses when you gain it.

## Official FAQ

- When you gain this, you may discard any number of Treasure cards from your
  hand, to gain that many Horses from their pile.
- Reveal the discarded Treasure cards.
- You do not have to discard anything, and cannot discard anything but
  Treasures.

## card text

+1 Card

+2 Actions

When you gain this, you may discard any number of Treasures, revealed, to gain
that many Horses.
