The Treasure Chest is an Artifact from Renaissance. It gains you a Gold every Buy phase, and comes into effect the turn you take it. There is one copy of it, and players may be able to take it from each other when playing a Swashbuckler.

see [swashbuckler](../kingdom/swashbuckler.md) for faq

## card text

At the start of your Buy phase, gain a Gold.
