Settlers is an Action card from Empires. It is a Peddler variant and a Split pile card, with 5 copies of Settlers sitting on top of Bustling Village.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

You may reveal a Copper from your discard pile and put it into your hand.

## Official FAQ

- You can look through your discard pile even if you know there is no Copper in it.
