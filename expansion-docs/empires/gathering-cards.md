Gathering is a card type introduced in Empires. Gathering cards collect VP tokens on their piles, which can be taken after a certain condition is met. Since the VP Gathered on Gathering card piles is a communal resource, all Gathering cards are also non-Attack interaction cards. The Gathering type acts as a reference point for Defiled Shrine.

## Official Rules

- A few Action cards have the type Gathering; this has no special functionality, but groups cards that add VP tokens to their Supply pile.
- VP tokens on a pile stay there until an ability specifically removes them; when gaining a card from that pile, slide the VP tokens off onto the next card.
- VP tokens can be on a pile even if no cards are left in the pile (players may use the randomizer cards to mark them).

## Additional rules clarifications

- VP tokens can still be added and removed from Gathering piles when not in the Supply, for example if Temple is chosen as the pile for Ferryman.
- If a Gathering card is used in the game without having a pile, for example if included in the Black Market deck or chosen for Way of the Mouse, no VP tokens can be placed or taken.
