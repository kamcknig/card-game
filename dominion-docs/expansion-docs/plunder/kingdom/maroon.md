Maroon is an Action card from Plunder. It is a trasher that provides terminal
draw; the amount of draw you get is increased by trashing cards with multiple
types.

## Official FAQ

Types are the words on the bottom banner of cards - Action, Attack, and so on.
For example if you trash a Cage with Maroon, you'll draw 4 cards, since it's a
Treasure and a Duration, 2 types.

## Other rules clarifications

If the trashed card has a Trait, or is Young Witch's Bane, that won't count as
an extra type for Maroon.
Inheritance, Capitalism, Charlatan, and Enlightenment will add types to cards.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand. +2 Cards per type it has (Action, Attack, etc.).
