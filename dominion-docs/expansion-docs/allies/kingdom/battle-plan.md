Battle Plan is an Action-Clash card from Allies. It provides non-terminal draw
if you have an Attack in your hand.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Clashes, Archer,
Warlord, and Territory; and it allows you to rotate the Clash cards, or any
other pile in the Supply—even Knights, Ruins, or split piles from Empires.

## Official FAQ

- First you get +1 Card and +1 Action, then you may reveal an Attack card
  from your hand to draw a card, and finally you may rotate any Supply pile.
- Many piles won't do anything meaningful if you do this. It can be relevant
  though for split piles, or for the Castles from Empires, or the Knights or
  Ruins from Dark Ages.

## Other rules clarifications

- If Clashes (or any other split pile) is chosen as the pile for Ferryman, it
  is not in the Supply, meaning that Battle Plan can't rotate it.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

You may reveal an Attack card from your hand for +1 Card.

You may rotate any Supply pile.
