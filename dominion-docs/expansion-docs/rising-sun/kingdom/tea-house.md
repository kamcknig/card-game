Tea House is an Action-Omen card from Rising Sun. It is a strong Peddler variant that gives $2, and as an Omen, it
also brings Prophecies closer to taking effect.

## Official FAQ

- First the +1Sun happens, which may trigger a Prophecy; then you get the +1 Card, +1 Action, and +$2.
