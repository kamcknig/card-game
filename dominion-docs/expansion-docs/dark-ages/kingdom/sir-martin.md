Sir Martin is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights, he
is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. He provides two
+Buys and is the cheapest Knight, with a cost of $4

## Official FAQ

- Sir Martin only costs $4, though the other Knights all cost $5. When
  Sir Martin is the top card of the pile, it can be gained with an Armory and so
  on.

## card text

+2 Buys

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them
costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this,
trash this.
