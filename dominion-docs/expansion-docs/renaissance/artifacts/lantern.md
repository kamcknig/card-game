The Lantern is an Artifact from Renaissance. It increases the amount of cards you reveal when playing Border Guard. This makes it easier to find a card you want to put into your hand, but harder to reveal only Actions to take the Horn. There is one copy of it, and players may be able to take it from each other when playing a Border Guard.

see [border guard](../kingdom/border-guard.md)

## card text

Border Guards you play reveal 3 cards and discard 2. (It takes all 3 being Actions to take the Horn.)
