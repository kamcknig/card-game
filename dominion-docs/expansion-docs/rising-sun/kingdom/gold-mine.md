Gold Mine is an Action card from Rising Sun. It is a cantrip source of +Buy that allows you to gain Gold at a discounted
price of 4D.

## Official FAQ

- You can gain a Gold even if you already have D; see the Debt section.
- You can't gain a Gold without taking 4D.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

+1 Buy

You may gain a Gold and get +4D.
