Kitsune is an Action-Attack-Omen card from Rising Sun. It provides you with a choice between several possible
combinations of effects, as a village, curser, Silver gainer, or source of virtual coin. As an Omen, it also brings
Prophecies closer to taking effect.

## Official FAQ

First the +1Sun happens, which may trigger a Prophecy; then you choose two different options, and do them in the order
listed.

## card text

+1Sun

Choose two different options: +2 Actions; +$2; each other player gains a Curse; gain a Silver.
