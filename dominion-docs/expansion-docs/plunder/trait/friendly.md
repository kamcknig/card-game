Friendly is a Trait from Plunder. Cards with this trait allow you to gain a free copy of them if you leave another copy
in hand until the start of your Clean-up phase.

## Official FAQ

You may only discard one Friendly card per turn this way.

## Other rules clarifications

If a split pile is Friendly, you can discard a Friendly card to gain a Friendly card with a different name (e.g. discard
a Battle Plan to gain a Warlord).
If Encampment is Friendly, you can first return a set-aside Encampment to its pile, and then discard an Encampment (or
Plunder) from your hand to regain it.

## card text

At the start of your Clean-up phase, you may discard a Friendly card to gain a Friendly card.
