Pursue is an Event from Menagerie which allows you some control over the top of
your deck by discarding any of the top 4 cards that do not match a named card.

## Official FAQ

- You can name a card that is not in the game; all four cards will be discarded.

## card text

+1 Buy

Name a card. Reveal the top 4 cards from your deck. Put the matches back and
discard the rest.
