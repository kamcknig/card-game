Tent is an Action-Fort card from Allies. It is a terminal silver that allows you
to top-deck it to reuse it turn after turn.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Forts, Garrison, Hill
Fort, and Stronghold, and allows you to rotate the pile if you want.

## Official FAQ

- If you have multiple Tents in play, you can choose how many you want to put
  on top of your deck.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you play this using a Way, or under the influence of Enchantress, you
  can still top-deck this when you discard it from play.

## card text

+$2

You may rotate the Forts.

When you discard this from play, you may put it onto your deck.
