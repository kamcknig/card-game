King's Cache is a Treasure card from Plunder. It allows you to play other
Treasure cards three times.

## Official FAQ

If you King's Cache a King's Cache, you will play three more Treasures three
times each.
If you King's Cache a Treasure-Duration card, King's Cache will stay in play as
long as that card does.

## Other rules clarifications

If you King's Cache a Capital, you end up with 6 debt when the Capital is
discarded, not 18.

## card text

You may play a Treasure from your hand 3 times.
