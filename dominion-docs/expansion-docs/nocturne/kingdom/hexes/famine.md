Famine is a Hex from Nocturne. It is a deck order attack that discards Actions from the top three cards of your deck, then shuffles your deck.

## Official FAQ

- The revealed cards that are not Actions are shuffled back into your deck.

## Other Rules clarifications

- When all revealed cards are Actions, you still shuffle your deck.
- If your deck is empty after you discard the Actions and you have no cards
  return to your deck, you simply do nothing—you do not shuffle your discard pile to form a new deck.

## card text

Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Discard the Actions. Shuffle the rest into your deck.
