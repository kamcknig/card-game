Supplies is a Treasure card from Menagerie. It is worth $1 like Copper, but you
gain a Horse which can normally be played during your next turn.

## Official FAQ

- This is a Treasure worth $1, like Copper; you play it in your Buy phase.
- When you play this, gain a Horse, putting it directly onto your deck.

## card text

$1

Gain a Horse onto your deck.
