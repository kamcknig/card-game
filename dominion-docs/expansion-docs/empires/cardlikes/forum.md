Forum is an Action card from Empires. It is a sifter that maintains hand size.

## card text

+3 Cards

+1 Action

Discard 2 cards.

When you gain this, +1 Buy.

Official FAQ (2016)

- For example, with $13 and only one Buy, you could buy a Forum, getting +1 Buy, then buy a Province.

Other rules clarifications

- Since 2022, Forum's +Buy bonus' trigger is changing from "when you buy" to "when you gain".
- Getting +1 Buy happens when you buy or otherwise gain this.
