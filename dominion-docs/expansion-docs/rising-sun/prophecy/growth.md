Growth is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it is activated, whenever anyone gains a Treasure they also gain an
additional card cheaper than the Treasure.

## Official FAQ

- This can chain; you could gain a Rice, gain a Gold due to Growth happening for Rice, gain a Silver due to Growth
  happening for Gold, then gain an Estate due to Growth happening for Silver.
- This is not optional; if you gain a Treasure, you have to gain a cheaper card if you can.

## card text

When you gain a Treasure, gain a cheaper card.
