Haunted Mirror is a Treasure-Heirloom card from Nocturne, replacing one of your starting Coppers when Cemetery is in the Kingdom. It's no better than a Copper when played, but if you trash it at the right time it can get you a Ghost.

## Official FAQ

- Haunted Mirror does not give you a way to trash it, but does something if
  you find a way to.

## card text

$1
When you trash this, you may discard an Action card, to gain a Ghost.
