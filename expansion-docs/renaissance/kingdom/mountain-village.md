Mountain Village is an Action card from Renaissance. It is a village that draws its card from the player's discard pile, if possible.

## Official FAQ

- If your discard pile has any cards in it, you have to take one of them,
  you cannot choose to draw a card instead.
- You get to look through your discard pile to pick the card to take.
- It does not matter what order you leave your discard pile in.

## card text

+2 Actions

Look through your discard pile and put a card from it into your hand; if you can't, +1 Card.
