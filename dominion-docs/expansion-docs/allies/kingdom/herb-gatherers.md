Herb Gatherer is an Action-Augur card from Allies. It offers +Buy, a deck
discarding effect like Chancellor, and allows you to play a Treasure during your
Action phase.

Herb Gatherer is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Augurs:
Acolyte, Sorceress, and Sibyl; and it allows rotating the split pile.

## Official FAQ

- Putting your deck into your discard pile does not trigger "when you discard
  this" abilities like Tunnel's (from Hinterlands).
- Playing a Treasure from your discard pile is optional, as is rotating the
  Augurs.

## card text

+1 Buy

Put your deck into your discard pile. Look through it and you may play a
Treasure from it.

You may rotate the Augurs.
