Augurs are a split pile from the Allies expansion. There are 4 copies each of 4
differently-named Augurs, with a single randomizer card whose type is
Action-Augur; when Augurs are selected as a Kingdom card for a game, they are
put into a single Supply pile in order by increasing cost, of which only the top
card can be gained at any given time. The cheapest of the four allows you to "
rotate" the pile—move all copies of the current top card to the bottom of the
pile, revealing the next card in the sequence.
