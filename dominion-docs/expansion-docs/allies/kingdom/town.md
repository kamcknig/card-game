Town is an Action card from Allies. It gives you a choice between the effects of
Village and Woodcutter, so you can use it as a source of either extra Actions or
+Buy.

## Official FAQ

- You simply choose to either get +1 Card and +2 Actions, or +1 Buy and +$2.

## card text

Choose one: +1 Card and +2 Actions; or +1 Buy and +$2.
