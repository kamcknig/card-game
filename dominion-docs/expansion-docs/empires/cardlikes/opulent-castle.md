Opulent Castle is an Action-Victory-Castle card from Empires. It is the fifth of eight different Castles that make up the Castles split pile and is a discard for benefit card that gives +$2 for each Victory card you discard to it.

## card text

Discard any number of Victory cards, revealed. +$2 per card discarded.

3VP
