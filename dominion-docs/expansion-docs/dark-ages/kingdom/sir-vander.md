Sir Vander is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights, he
is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. When played, he
does nothing else; however, when he is trashed, you gain a Gold.

## Official FAQ

- If Sir Vander is trashed, you gain a Gold; this happens whether it is
  trashed on your turn or someone else's. The player who had Sir Vander is the
  one who gains the Gold, regardless of who played the card that trashed it.

## card text

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them
costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this,
trash this.

When you trash this, gain a Gold.
