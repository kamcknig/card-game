Kintsugi is an Event from Rising Sun. It starts as a simple trasher, but after you gain a Gold it becomes a remodeler.

## Official FAQ

You have to remember if you gained a Gold this game. If you have, buying Kintsugi will both trash and gain a card, even
if you no longer have the Gold.

## Other rules clarifications

If trashing a card causes you to gain a Gold (e.g. you discard a Market Square), that will let you gain a card costing
up to $2 more.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand. If you've gained a Gold this game, gain a card costing up to $2 more than the trashed card.
