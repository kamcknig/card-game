Wild Hunt is an Action-Gathering card from Empires. It is a terminal draw card that Gathers VP tokens when played; the player can choose to forgo the draw to instead gain an Estate and take the Gathered VP.

## card text

Choose one: +3 Cards and add 1VP to the Wild Hunt pile; or gain an Estate, and if you do, take the VP from the pile.

## Official FAQ

- If the Estate pile is empty, you can choose that option but will not get the VP tokens.
- Wild Hunt still functions normally if the Wild Hunt pile is empty.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you obtain Wild Hunt from the Black Market deck, it has no pile and cannot gather VP tokens. The same is true if the Wild Hunt pile is eliminated by Divine Wind. You can still gain an Estate if Wild Hunt has no pile, but there will be no VP tokens to take.
