Aristocrat is an Action card from Rising Sun. It provides +3 of any of the vanilla bonuses, but which one it provides
depends on how many Aristocrats are in play. It starts as a non-drawing, and therefore weak, village; the second
Aristocrat gives terminal draw; the third provides +$$; the fourth gives +Buy, and then the sequence repeats.

## Official FAQ

- What matters is how many Aristocrats you have in play, not how many you played that turn. For example if you play
  Daimyo and then Aristocrat, you'll get +3 Actions for each play.
- If you have zero or 9 or 10 Aristocrats in play, it doesn't do anything.

## Other rules clarifications

- Command variants leave the card where it is, so an Aristocrat played that way will not be considered in play. If
  that's the first time Aristocrat has been played, there will be zero in play, so it won't do anything.

## card text

If the number of Aristocrats you have in play is:

1 or 5: +3 Actions;

2 or 6: +3 Cards;

3 or 7: +$3;

4 or 8: +3 Buys.
