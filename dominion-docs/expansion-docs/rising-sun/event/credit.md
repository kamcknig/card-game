Credit is an Event from Rising Sun. In effect, it allows you to buy Action or Treasure cards for DD instead of $, but
it comes with an up-front additional payment of $2.

## Official FAQ

- This can't gain cards with D in the cost.

## Additional rules clarifications

- It also can't gain cards with P in the cost.
- If you play Possession and have them buy Credit, they don't gain the card (you do), which means there's no D given to
  any player.
- If the gained card's cost changes when you gain it (e.g., the card is Destrier), take DD based on the new cost, not
  the old cost.
- If its cost somehow changes to include D or P (e.g. Credit gains a $6 Wayfarer, use Architects' Guild to gain
  Gondola and play Lurker to gain either Transmute or Artist), only the $ of the new cost matters; the D and P are
  ignored.

## card text

Gain an Action or Treasure costing up to $8. +D equal to its cost.
