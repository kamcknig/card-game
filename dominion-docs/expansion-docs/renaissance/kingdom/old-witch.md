Old Witch is an Action-Attack card from Renaissance. It draws 3 cards, then hands out Curses like Witch, but also lets opponents trash a Curse from their hand.

## Official FAQ

- After the Curse pile is empty, playing this still lets each other player
  trash a Curse from their hand.
- A player who is unaffected by Old Witch, such as due to Moat, neither
  gains a Curse nor may trash one.

## Other rules clarifications

- The Curse is gained before you may trash one. So you can gain a Curse,
  react with Sheepdog, draw a Curse, and then trash that Curse.

## card text

+3 Cards

Each other player gains a Curse and may trash a Curse from their hand.
