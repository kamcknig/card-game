Shield is a Treasure-Reaction-Loot card from Plunder. Like most Loot cards, it provides +$3 and +Buy; its distinguishing
feature is that it also protects you from Attacks like a Moat.

## Official FAQ

You can reveal this when another player plays an Attack card to be unaffected by it, exactly as with Moat.
You do this before the Attack card has done anything, and can use Shield against multiple Attacks in a turn.
Shield stays in your hand and can still be played for +$3 and +1 Buy on your turn.

## card text

$3

+1 Buy

When another player plays an Attack, you may first reveal this from your hand to be unaffected.
