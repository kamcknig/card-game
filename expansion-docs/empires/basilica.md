Basilica is a Landmark from Empires. It holds a finite number of VP tokens, which players take from when they have at least $2 left after gaining a card.

## card text

When you gain a card in your Buy phase, if you have $2 or more, take 2VP from here.

Setup: Put 6VP here per player.

## Unofficial FAQ (2022)

- This happens each time you gain a card in your Buy phase, and works on cards gained via buying them, and cards gained other ways.
- For example with $4 and 3 Buys, you could buy Copper, then Copper, then Silver, taking 2VP, then 2VP, then none. And if you play a Cursed Gold, you'll get 2VP from the Curse.
- You do not get 2VP for buying an Event or Project, but if the Event gains you cards (such as Advance), that can let you get VP from this.
- If you gain a card while you have \$0, and gaining the card causes you to get +\$2 (e.g. you gained a Nomads), that will let you take 2VP from this.
- If you gain a card that lets you overpay (e.g. Stonemason), you have to pay the extra $ before you gain it. This could prevent you from getting the VP from this.
