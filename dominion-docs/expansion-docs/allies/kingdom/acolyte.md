Acolyte is an Action-Augur card from Allies. It allows you to gain Gold by
trashing your Action or Victory cards.

It is part of a split pile which it shares with the other Augurs: Herb Gatherer,
Sorceress, and Sibyl, and its second ability helps you gain those cards.

## Official FAQ

- Both abilities are optional; you may do either or both or neither.
- You only gain a Gold if you actually trashed an Action or Victory card from
  your hand; you only gain an Augur if you actually trashed Acolyte.
- Gaining an Augur will give you whichever Augur is on top of the pile
  currently, even if that's another Acolyte.

## card text

You may trash an Action or Victory card from your hand to gain a Gold.

You may trash this to gain an Augur.
