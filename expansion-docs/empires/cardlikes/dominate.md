Dominate is an Event from Empires. It gives the player a Province plus 9VP tokens, for a total of 15VP. It has the most expensive $ cost of any card or Event in the game, and can be thought of as the next step up on the basic Victory card chain from Colony.

## card text

Gain a Province. If you do, +9VP.

## Official FAQ

- This does nothing once the Province pile is empty.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you exchange the Province with a Silver or a Changeling, you still get +9VP. But if you Possess a player and make them buy Dominate, no one gets +9VP.
