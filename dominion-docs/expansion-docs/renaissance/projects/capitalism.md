Capitalism is a Project from Renaissance. Once you've bought it, on your turns Action cards that produce +$ acquire the Treasure type. This allows these Action cards to be played non-terminally during your Buy phase and to interact with cards that care about Treasures, such as Mine.

## Official FAQ

- To be affected, a card must have a +\$ amount in its text, not just a \$
  amount - for example, Capitalism turns Improve into a Treasure, but does not affect Inventor.
- Having Capitalism means you can play any number of Action cards with +
  amounts in your Buy phase (without using up Action plays).
- It also means that things that interact with Treasure cards also interact
  with those cards; for example, if you have Capitalism, you can use Treasurer to gain an Improve from the trash, since Improve is a Treasure on your turns.
- Any time you play an Action - Treasure card, it is both an Action and a
  Treasure, regardless of which phase it is.
- Getting +1 Action in your Buy phase does not let you play other Action
  cards then.
- Capitalism works on your turn, but affects cards everywhere; for example
  if you have Capitalism and play Bandit, you could trash another player's Improve, and it is not relevant if that player has Capitalism or not.

## Other rules clarifications

- A card with +\$ in its text becomes a Treasure even if it does not give you
  +$ when you play it. For instance:
- Aristocrat only gives you +$3 under relatively restricted circumstances
  depending on how many other Aristocrats are in play, but if you have Capitalism, Aristocrat is a Treasure at all times during your turn, regardless of how many Aristocrats are in play.
- With Capitalism you could play a Steward during your Buy phase and use it
  to trash two cards, receiving no +$.
- Caravan Guard becomes a Treasure even though it only gives +$ on a future
  turn.
- Things that are not Action cards do not become Treasures even if they have
  +$ in their text, such as Raider.
- Druid does not become a Treasure even if one of the Boons it controls
  gives +$; what matters is the text of the Druid card itself. Similarly, a Way that gives +$ such as Way of the Seal has no effect on which cards are subject to Capitalism.
- Since Capitalism changes the number of types a card has, that will affect
  how it interacts with cards such as Courtier and Falconer.

## card text

During your turns, Actions with +$ amounts in their text are also Treasures.
