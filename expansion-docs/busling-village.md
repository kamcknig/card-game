Bustling Village is an Action card from Empires. It is a village, a Lab variant, and a Split pile card, with 5 copies sitting under 5 copies of Settlers. It is the only card to give +3 Actions unconditionally.

## card text

+1 Card

+3 Actions

You may reveal a Settlers from your discard pile and put it into your hand.

## Official FAQ

- You can look through your discard pile even if you know there are no Settlers in it.
