Sacred Grove is an Action-Fate card from Nocturne. It's a terminal Gold with +Buy that also gives you a Boon, which other players may also receive.

## Official FAQ

- You have to receive the Boon; the other players can choose to receive it.
- The Field's Gift and The Forest's Gift are not shared.
- The River's Gift means that each player choosing to receive it draws a card
  at the end of your turn, at the same time as you.

## card text

+1 Buy

+$3

Receive a Boon. If it doesn't give +$1, each other player may receive it.
