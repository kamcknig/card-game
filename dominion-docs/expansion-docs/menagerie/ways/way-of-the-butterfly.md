Way of the Butterfly is a Way from Menagerie. It is similar to Upgrade in that
it gains a card costing $1$1 more than another card, but instead of trashing the
card, it simply returns it to its pile.

## Official FAQ

- You only gain a card if you manage to return the card to its pile. A
  non-Supply card (like Horse) can return to its pile; a card with no pile, such
  as Necropolis (from Dark Ages), fails to return to its pile.
- The card you gain comes from the Supply, and can be any type; if there is
  no card in the Supply costing exactly $1$1 more than the returned card, you do
  not gain one.

## Other rules clarifications

- Cards bought from the Black Market deck do not come from a pile, so Way of
  the Butterfly has no effect on them.

## card text

You may return this to its pile to gain a card costing exactly $1$1 more than
it.
