Envy is a Hex from Nocturne. It is a turn-worsening attack that effectively turns your Silvers and Golds into Coppers for a turn. It uses the State Envious to track this. Its effect cannot stack with Delusion's.

## Official FAQ

- Deluded/Envious is two-sided; take it with the Envious side face up.

## card text

If you don't have Deluded or Envious, take Envious.
