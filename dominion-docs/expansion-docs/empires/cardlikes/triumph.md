Triumph is an Event from Empires. For 5D, it gives you an Estate and a VP token for each card you've gained that turn.

## card text

Gain an Estate. If you did, +1VP per card you've gained this turn.

## Official FAQ

- You get +1VP per card you have gained, including the Estate, and any other cards bought or gained other ways; you do not get VP for Events bought.
- Once the Estate pile is empty, this does nothing.

## Other rules clarifications

- After gaining an Estate from this, if you gain a Curse from another player's Black Cat, that counts as a card you've gained this turn.
