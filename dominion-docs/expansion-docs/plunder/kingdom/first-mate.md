First Mate is an Action card from Plunder. It allows you to play multiple copies
of the same Action card without using up extra Actions. It also is a Draw-to-X
card, drawing your hand back up to 6 cards after you've played your duplicate
Action cards.

## Official FAQ

If you don't have any Action cards to play, you'll still draw up to 6.
If the Action card you play draws you another copy of itself, you can play that
copy, and so on.
First Mate can play First Mates; keep careful track of which card you're
resolving, as you would with multiple Throne Rooms.

## card text

Play any number of Action cards with the same name from your hand, then draw
until you have 6 cards in hand.
