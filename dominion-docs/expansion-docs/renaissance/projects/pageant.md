Pageant is a Project from Renaissance. It lets you convert an unspent $1 into a Coffers token.

## Official FAQ

- If you have at least \$1 that you did not spend, you can spend $1 for +1
  Coffers.
- This only works once per turn.

## Other rules clarification

- If you have \$2 unspent, you can discard a Wine Merchant from your Tavern
  mat, and then pay $1 for Pageant.
- If your turn has multiple Buy phases (due to Villa or Cavalry), you can pay
  $1 to Pageant at the end of each of those Buy phases.

## card text

At the end of your Buy phase, you may pay $1 for +1 Coffers.
