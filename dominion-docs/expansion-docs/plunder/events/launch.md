Launch is an Event from Plunder. It allows you to spend $3 to go back to your Action phase from your Buy phase.

## Official FAQ

This ends your Buy phase and returns you to your Action phase.
This does not cause "start of turn" abilities to repeat; however when your Buy phase happens again after that, "start of
Buy phase" abilities can repeat.

## Other rules clarifications

"Once per turn" applies to the whole Event.
This counts as ending your Buy phase (for cards like Wine Merchant and Pageant). If you take multiple Buy phases, those
cards will trigger multiple times.
Unlike Cavalry, Launch draws you a card after ending your Buy phase. So if ending your Buy phase makes you put a
Treasury onto your deck, you will draw it with Launch.

## card text

Once per turn: Return to your Action phase. +1 Card, +1 Action, and +1 Buy.
