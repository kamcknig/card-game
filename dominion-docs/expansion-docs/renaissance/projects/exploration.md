Exploration is a Project from Renaissance. It rewards you with +1 Coffers and +1 Villager for ending your Buy phase without gaining any cards during it.

## Unofficial FAQ (2022)

- This only cares if you gained a card in your Buy phase, whether bought, or
  gained other ways, such as via the Treasure Chest.
- It does not count cards gained in your Action phase, such as with Inventor.
- For example if all you buy on your turn is Exploration, you will get +1
  Coffers and +1 Villager that turn.
- In rare cases (such as with Villa, from Empires), your Buy phase can end
  multiple times in a turn; Exploration will happen each time it ends.

## card text

At the end of your Buy phase, if you didn't gain any cards during it, +1 Coffers and +1 Villager.
