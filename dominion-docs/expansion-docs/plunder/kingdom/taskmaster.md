Taskmaster is an Action-Duration card from Plunder. It will give you an extra Action next turn if you gain a card
costing $5$5 this turn, and it will remain in play giving you extra Actions for as many turns as you keep gaining $5's.

## Official FAQ

Taskmaster can end up making +1 Action and +$1 turn after turn, as long as you keep gaining at least one card
costing $5.
It only matters what the card cost when you gained it, not what it costs at other times.
Taskmaster does not count cards gained before playing it.

## Other rules clarifications

Repeating Taskmaster's ability doesn't count as playing it again (which means you can't use a Way to make Taskmaster do
something other than its usual ability at this point, and it won't count for Conspirator).
At the start of your turn, you can first repeat this ability, then gain a Duchy with Importer, and that will let the
Taskmaster repeat itself on your next turn.
This checks the cost that a card had at the moment you gained it. So if you gain a Destrier that costs $6, that won't
count for Taskmaster (even though its own gain means it now costs $5). But if you gain a Destrier that costs $5 (
meaning it now costs $4), that lets Taskmaster repeat itself.
If gaining a $5-cost card causes you to play a Taskmaster (e.g. you play Haggler and buy a $5 card, and then gain a
Taskmaster with Haggler's ability and play the Taskmaster with Innovation), that will let the Taskmaster repeat itself
on your next turn.

## card text

+1 Action, +$1, and if you gain a card costing exactly $5 this turn, then at the start of your next turn, repeat this
ability.
