Poet is an Action-Omen card from Rising Sun. It is a cantrip that can turn into non-terminal draw if the next card in
your deck costs $3 or less. As an Omen, it also brings Prophecies closer to taking effect.

## Official FAQ

- Cards with D in their costs do not cost "$3 or less."
- The card goes back on top of your deck if it doesn't get put into your hand.

## card text

+1Sun

+1 Card

+1 Action

Reveal the top card of your deck. If it costs $3 or less, put it into your hand.
