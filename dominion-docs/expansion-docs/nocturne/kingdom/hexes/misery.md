Misery is a Hex from Nocturne. It is the only Attack-style effect that only hurts your score, giving you -2 VP when you first receive it, or -4 VP total if you've received it at least twice. It uses the States Miserable and Twice Miserable to track this.

## Official FAQ

- If this hits you for a third time in a game, nothing will happen; you stay
  at Twice Miserable.

## card text

If this is your first Misery this game, take Miserable. Otherwise, flip it over to Twice Miserable.
