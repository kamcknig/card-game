Alliance is an Event from Menagerie. It allows you to gain one of each Basic
card (except Curse) with just one buy for $10$10.

## Official FAQ

- You gain each of those cards that is present in the Supply; you cannot
  choose not to gain one. You gain them in the order listed.

## card text

Gain a Province, a Duchy, an Estate, a Gold, a Silver, and a Copper.
