Triumphal Arch is a Landmark from Empires. It rewards players with VP for accumulating lots of copies of at least two different Action cards.

## card text

When scoring, 3VP per copy you have of the 2nd most common Action card among your cards (if it's a tie, count either).

## Official FAQ

- For example, if you had 7 copies of Villa and 4 copies of Wild Hunt, you would score 12VP.

## Other Rules clarifications

- The tie can be for either first or second place: if you had 7 copies each of Villa and Village, you would score 21VP. If you had 7 copies of Villa, then 4 copies each of Village and Wild Hunt, you would score 12VP.
