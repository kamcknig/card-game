Bureaucracy is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it is activated, players must also gain Copper whenever they gain a more
expensive card, thus rapidly junking their deck as they try to improve it.

## Official FAQ

- This includes cards gained from outside the Supply (such as Loot, from Plunder).
- Cards with DD costs don't cost exactly $0, and so come with a Copper.

## card text

When you gain a card that doesn't cost $0, gain a Copper.
