Grand Castle is a Victory-Castle card from Empires. It is the seventh of eight different Castles that make up the Castles split pile and can potentially score a lot through its on-gain  VP token gain.

## card text

5VP

When you gain this, reveal your hand. +1VP per Victory card in your hand and/or in play

## Official FAQ

- Grand Castle counts both Victory cards in play - such as an Opulent Castle - and Victory cards in your hand.

## Other Rules clarifications

- This counts all Victory cards in play. So if another player has a Stronghold in play, you'll get +1 VP for it. [1]
