Stampede is an Event from Menagerie that allows you to gain 5 Horses directly
onto your deck, but only if you can get $5$5 without playing more than 5 cards.
This effectively doubles the size of your next hand, since once you play all
five Horses, you'll have 10 cards.

## Official FAQ

- It does not matter how many cards you played that turn, only how many you
  have in play when buying Stampede. If there are fewer than 5 Horses left in
  the pile, just gain as many as you can.

## card text

If you have 5 or fewer cards in play, gain 5 Horses onto your deck.
