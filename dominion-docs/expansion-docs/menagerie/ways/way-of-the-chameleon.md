Way of the Chameleon is a Way from Menagerie. It lets you turn an Action card's
+Cards into +$$, and its +$$ into +Cards.

## Official FAQ

- For example, if you play Sheepdog and use Way of the Chameleon, you will
  get +$2$2 instead of +2 Cards.
- If you play a Duration card using Way of the Chameleon, only the +$$ and
  +Cards you get that turn are affected; for example, if you play Merchant
  Ship (from Seaside) and use Way of the Chameleon, you will get +2 Cards this
  turn, but the normal +$2$2 next turn.
- This turns "+Cards" into "+$$" and vice-versa, but does not change other
  ways to draw cards, for example, "draw until you have 6 cards in hand."
- If the card that uses Way of the Chameleon plays another card, that card
  just does what it normally does (unless you use Way of the Chameleon on it as
  well).

## Other rules clarifications

- Different printings of Cellar, Oracle, Storeroom, and Storyteller have
  inconsistent uses of "+Cards" and "draw." Go by whichever wording is printed
  on the cards you are using (only +Cards is affected by Way of the Chameleon).
- When Way of the Chameleon turns +Cards into +$$, you do not remove your –1
  Card token; when it turns +$$ into +Cards, you do not remove your –$1$1 token.
- Enchantress, Highwayman, and Enlightenment can change what a card does;
  using Way of the Chameleon does not stop the new effect [1], or apply to what
  you get from it [2]. For example, if you play a Smithy using Way of the
  Chameleon but it is affected by Enchantress, you get +1 Card and +1 Action.
- Harbor Village only cares about whether your next Action card gave you +$$
  after Way of the Chameleon affected it.
- After Enlightenment happens, if you use Way of the Chameleon on Treasures
  outside of Action phases, it turns their +$$ (including the default $$ amounts
  on Treasures like Silver and Stockpile) into +Cards.
- The Lantern, Elder, and Reckless still apply to cards played using Way of
  the Chameleon. [3]
- Way of the Chameleon only affects abilities that happen because the card is
  played (i.e. those above a dividing line), but affects them even if they don't
  happen until later this turn. For example, using Improve to trash a Nomads you
  played using Way of the Chameleon gives you +$2$2, but trashing a card after
  playing a Priest using Way of the Chameleon gives you +2 Cards.
- Way of the Chameleon does not affect abilities other players get from the
  card. For example, the first option of Governor under Way of the Chameleon
  gives the other players +1 Card and you +$3$3.
- Way of the Chameleon has no effect on –$$ abilities (like those on Poor
  House and Souk), +Coffers abilities, the vanilla bonus tokens, or landscape
  cards like Boons and Allies.
- Any Action card can be played using Way of the Chameleon, even if would
  make no difference (e.g. Chapel or Market).

## card text

Follow this card's instructions; each time that would give you +Cards this turn,
you get +$$ instead, and vice-versa.
