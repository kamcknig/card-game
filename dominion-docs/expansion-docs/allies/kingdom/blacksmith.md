Blacksmith is an Action-Townsfolk card from Allies. It gives you a choice of two
types of terminal draw; and if you don't want either you can use it as a
cantrip.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Townsfolk: Town Crier,
Miller, and Elder.

## Official FAQ

- You either draw until you have 6 cards in hand, or draw 2 cards, or draw
  one card and get +1 Action.

## card text

Choose one: Draw until you have 6 cards in hand; or +2 Cards; or +1 Card and +1
Action.
