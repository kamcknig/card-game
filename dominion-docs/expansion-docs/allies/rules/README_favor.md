Favors, introduced in Allies, are coin tokens a player accumulates on their
Favors mat. Each player begins the game with one Favor when one or more Liaison
cards are in the Kingdom, and can collect more via the Liaison's effects. In any
game in which Favors are in use, an Ally is selected as part of game setup; the
Ally determines the rules for how and when Favors are used in that game and what
their effect is.

Most Allies, such as Peaceful Cult, require players to spend Favor tokens to
activate their effects; a few, such as League of Bankers, provide benefits while
leaving Favor tokens on the mat.

## Official FAQ

- Coin tokens are used for Favors; they go on a Favors mat to distinguish
  them from Coffers and Villagers (from other expansions), which have their own
  mats.
- When a card gives you +1 Favor, add a token to your mat; when spending a
  Favor, remove the token from your mat.
- Favors may be used starting with the first turn of the game; they may not
  be used prior to that turn.
- Spending Favors is always optional.
- Spending Favors can only be done once per time an Ally ability triggers,
  unless it says, "Repeat as desired."
