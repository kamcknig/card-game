Band of Nomads is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to spend a Favor for a
small bonus when you gain a card costing $3$3 or more; this can be used, for
example, to make a gainer non-terminal or give yourself an extra +Buy when you
need one.

## Official FAQ

- What matters is how much the card costs when you gain it, not how much it
  normally costs. This only works once per gain; you can spend a Favor, and then
  get your choice of +1 Card, +1 Action, or +1 Buy.

## Other rules clarifications

- The card has to cost $3$3 or more when you gain it; if it changes cost
  afterwards, that doesn't matter. For example, if you gain a Fisherman when it
  costs $2$2, and it goes into your discard pile, you can't spend a Favor for
  Band of Nomads, even though by that time the Fisherman now costs $5$5.
- Unlike City-state, you may spend Favors for this when gaining a card during
  another player's turn (with e.g. Barbarian). Getting +1 Action or +1 Buy is
  not useful.

## card text

When you gain a card costing $3 or more, you may spend a Favor, for +1 Card, or
+1 Action, or +1 Buy.
