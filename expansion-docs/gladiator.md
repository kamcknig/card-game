Gladiator is an Action card from Empires. It's a terminal silver that becomes a terminal gold if you can choose a card in your hand that your opponent can't match. It is a split pile card, with five copies of Gladiator sitting on top of five copies of Fortune; Gladiator can also trash other copies of Gladiator from the supply to help you get to Fortune faster.

## card text

+\$2

Reveal a card from your hand. The player to your left may reveal a copy from their hand. If they don't, +$1 and trash a Gladiator from its pile.

## Official FAQ

- If there are no Gladiators in the Supply, you cannot trash one, but that does not stop you from getting the +$1.
- If you have no cards in hand, the player to your left cannot reveal a copy of the card you revealed, so you will get the +$1 and trash a Gladiator.

## Other rules clarifications

- You can't trash a Gladiator from its pile if it's not the top card of the pile (e.g. due to rotating the pile with Battle Plan).
