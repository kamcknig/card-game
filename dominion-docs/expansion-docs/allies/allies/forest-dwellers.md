Forest Dwellers is an Ally from Allies. It is a sifter, allowing you to spend
Favor tokens to discard bad cards from your deck at the start of your turn.

## Official FAQ

- You can only do this once per turn.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you happen to put a card on top of your deck in the middle of resolving
  Forest Dwellers—for instance, by discarding a Tunnel, gaining a Gold, and
  top-decking it due to Progress—then when you return any remaining cards to
  your deck they will go on top of the card you just put there.

## card text

At the start of your turn, you may spend a Favor to look at the top 3 cards of
your deck, discard any number and put the rest back in any order.
