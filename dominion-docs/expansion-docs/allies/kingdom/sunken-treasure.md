Sunken Treasure is a Treasure-Odyssey card from Allies. It is a gainer that can
gain any Action card from the Supply, with no cost restrictions, as long as you
don't already have a copy of the card you want to gain in play.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Odyssey cards: Old Map,
Voyage, and Distant Shore.

## Official FAQ

- If there's no such Action in the Supply, you don't gain one.

## card text

Gain an Action card you don't have a copy of in play.
