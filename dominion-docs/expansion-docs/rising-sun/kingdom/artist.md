Artist is an Action card from Rising Sun. It provides a variable amount of non-terminal draw, depending on what cards
you have in play: it draws one card per unique card in play, making it strong if you have a large variety of cards; on
the other hand, if you have no unique cards in play it draws nothing at all!

## Official FAQ

- This costs 8D; see the Debt section.
- This counts itself, if you have exactly one Artist in play.
- This counts cards played on other turns that are still in play, such as a Samurai from a previous turn.

## card text

+1 Action

+1 Card per card you have exactly one copy of in play.
