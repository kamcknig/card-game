Crucible is a Treasure card from Plunder. It is a trash-for-benefit card that gives you +$$ equal to the cost of the trashed card, like Salvager. Unlike Salvager, Crucible is a Treasure (and therefore non-terminal), but it doesn't give +Buy.

## Official FAQ
For example if you trash an Estate, which costs $2 you get +$2.
If you trash a card with PP or DD in its cost (from other expansions), you get nothing for those symbols.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand. +$1 per $1 it costs.
