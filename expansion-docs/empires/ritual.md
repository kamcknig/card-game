Ritual is an Event from Empires. It allows the buyer to trash a card in exchange for VP tokens equal to its cost, but offsets the potentially large VP payoff by replacing the trashed card with a Curse

## card text

Gain a Curse. If you do, trash a card from your hand. +1VP per $1 it cost.

## Official FAQ

- This does nothing once the Curse pile is empty.
- This only gives you +1VP per $1 the trashed card cost; it does not give anything for D or P in costs.

## Other rules clarifications

- Since 2025, Ritual cares about the cost of the card after it has been trashed. For example, if you trash a Wayfarer (which costs $0 from the Curse), then discard a Market Square to gain a Gold, Ritual will give 6VP and not 0VP since Wayfarer now costs \$6.
