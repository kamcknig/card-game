Bury is an Event from Plunder. It allows you to retrieve a card from your discard pile to put at the bottom of your
deck, so you'll probably draw it earlier than you otherwise would.

## Official FAQ

Once you buy this, the ability is mandatory.

## Other rules clarifications

You cannot search through your discard pile prior to buying this Event to check if you want to buy it.

## card text

+1 Buy

Put any card from your discard pile on the bottom of your deck.
