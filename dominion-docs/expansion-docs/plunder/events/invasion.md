Invasion is an Event from Plunder. It allows you to gain a number of potentially-valuable cards at once — a Duchy, a
Loot card, and any Action of your choice — while also playing an Attack card if you choose.

## Official FAQ

You do the four things in that order.
Playing an Attack card is optional; the rest are mandatory.

## Other rules clarifications

When you gain a Loot and play it, the No Visiting rule is not in effect; you gain the Loot to your discard pile, and
then any on-gain triggers are activated, and then you play the Loot. If an on-gain trigger moves the Loot out of your
discard pile (for example, you reveal a Watchtower to top-deck the Loot, the stop-moving rule prevents you from playing
it.

## card text

You may play an Attack from your hand. Gain a Duchy. Gain an Action onto your deck. Gain a Loot; play it.
