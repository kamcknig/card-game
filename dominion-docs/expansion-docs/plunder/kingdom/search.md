Search is an Action-Duration card from Plunder. It is a one-shot terminal silver that acts as a delayed Loot gainer. It
waits in play until a pile in the Supply is emptied; once that happens, you get a Loot card — and so does anybody else
who's played a Search.

## Official FAQ

If you Throne Room a Search, Throne Room will stay out with Search until a pile empties, and then you'll trash Search
once but gain two Loots (and discard Throne Room that turn).

## Other rules clarifications

See the Additional rules section for Duration cards in Dominion: Plunder regarding things happening "the next time".
If multiple players have played a Search when a Supply pile empties, players trash their Searches and gain Loots in turn
order.
It doesn't matter if the Supply pile had already been emptied on a previous turn. So if you empty a pile, return copies
to the Supply (with e.g. Swap), and empty it again, Search can trigger off that pile again.
If a non-supply pile (like Loot or Horse) is emptied, that won't trigger Search.
If gaining a card empties a Supply pile, you'll order Search with other when-gain effects. If trashing a card empties a
Supply pile (e.g. Lurker), you'll order Search with other when-trash effects.
If you Invest in the last card of a Supply pile, other players who also Invested in that card can order between the +2
Cards from Invest and trashing their Searches.
If emptying a Supply pile causes you to play this (e.g. you gain the last Gondola which lets you play a Search), that
won't trigger the Search (it triggers off the next Supply pile that's emptied).
Ending the game by emptying the third Supply pile, Provinces, or Colonies does trigger Search; you gain the Loot before
the game ends. Usually this won't make a difference, but trashing Search and gaining Loot can affect your score due to
abilities such as Fairgrounds, Tomb, and Keep, and there are a few abilities that might allow you to play the gained
Loot before the end of your final turn and use it to buy some additional VP.

## card text

+$2
The next time a Supply pile empties, trash this and gain a Loot.
