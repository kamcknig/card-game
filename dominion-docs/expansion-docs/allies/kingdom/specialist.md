Specialist is an Action card from Allies. It can be used as either a Throne Room
variant or a gainer, to either gain a copy of or get an extra play out of a card
in your hand.

## Official FAQ

- First you may play an Action or Treasure card from your hand. If you did,
  then after completely resolving playing that card, you choose to either play
  it again, or gain a copy of it.
- You can play the card again even if it left play.
- You can choose to gain a copy even if there are no copies left; you won't
  gain anything though.
- This can only gain cards from the Supply.

## card text

You may play an Action or Treasure from your hand. Choose one: Play it again; or
gain a copy of it.
