Patrician is an Action card from Empires. It provides non-terminal draw, if you happen to find a card costing $5 or more to draw. It's part of a split pile, with 5 copies of Patrician sitting on top of 5 copies of Emporium.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

Reveal the top card of your deck. If it costs $5 or more, put it into your hand.

# Official FAQ

- Cards costing D do not cost \$5 or more unless they also have a \$ cost of \$5 or more. So Fortune does but City Quarter does not.
