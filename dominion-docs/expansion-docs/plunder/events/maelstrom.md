Maelstrom is an Event from Plunder. It is a trasher that also trashes cards from your opponents' hands—which may either
help or hurt them.

## Official FAQ

This isn't optional for the other players; they must trash a card if they have 5 or more cards in hand.

## card text

Trash 3 cards from your hand. Each other player with 5 or more cards in hand trashes one of them.
