Small Castle is an Action-Victory-Castle card from Empires. It is the third of eight different Castles that make up the Castles split pile and allows you to trash a Castle you already have to gain a more powerful Castle.

## card text

Trash this or a Castle from your hand. If you do, gain a Castle.

2VP

## Official FAQ

- Small Castle gains you the top Castle, whichever one that is.

## Other Rules clarifications

- You are allowed to choose to trash a Castle from your hand, even if you don't have any Castles in your hand, though this means you will not gain a Castle.
