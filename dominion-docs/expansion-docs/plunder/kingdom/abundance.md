Abundance is a Treasure-Duration card from Plunder. When you play it, it does
nothing until you gain an Action card; at that point you get +$3$3 and +Buy.

## Official FAQ

This triggers when you gain an Action card due to buying it, or gain one
some other way.
If it happens during another player's turn, the +$3 and +1 Buy won't be
useful.

## Other rules clarifications

See the Additional rules section for Duration cards in Dominion: Plunder
regarding things happening "the next time".
If gaining an Action card causes you to play this (e.g. you gain a Courier and
play it with Innovation), that won't trigger the Abundance (it triggers off the
next Action you gain).

## card text

The next time you gain an Action card: +1 Buy and +$3.
