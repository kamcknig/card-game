Arena is a Landmark from Empires. It holds a finite number of VP tokens, which players take from if they keep an unplayed Action in hand to discard at the start of their Buy phase.

## card text

At the start of your Buy phase, you may discard an Action card. If you do, take 2VP from here.

Setup: Put 6VP here per player.

## Official FAQ

- With Villa it is possible for your Buy phase to start twice or more in a turn; you can make use of Arena each time.

## Other rules clarifications

- You can discard an Action card even if there's no more VP on this, which can be useful with cards like Village Green that do something special when discarded.
