Sir Michael is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights, he
is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. He is also a
discard attack, forcing other players to discard down to 3 cards in hand.

## Official FAQ

- Sir Michael causes players to discard before it trashes cards.

## card text

Each other player discards down to 3 cards in hand. Each other player reveals
the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them costing from $3 to $6, and
discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this, trash this.
