Pilgrim is an Action card from Plunder. Like a more powerful Courtyard, it
provides large terminal draw and requires you to put a card from your hand on
top of your deck; this may enable you to set up a good draw for your next turn,
or put back an Action card you've drawn dead.

## Official FAQ

The card you put on top doesn't have to be one of the 4 you just drew.

## card text

+4 Cards

Put a card from your hand onto your deck.
