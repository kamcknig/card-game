War is a Hex from Nocturne. It is a trashing attack, digging for a card in your deck costing $3 or $4 to trash.

## Official FAQ

- If you do not find a card costing $3 or $4, your entire deck will end up
  in your discard pile, with nothing trashed.

## card text

Reveal cards from your deck until revealing one costing $3 or $4. Trash it and discard the rest.
