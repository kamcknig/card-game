Aqueduct is a Landmark from Empires. It acts kind of like a Gathering card, accumulating VP tokens as players gain Treasures, and then giving them to a player when they gain a Victory card.

## card text

When you gain a Treasure, move 1VP from its pile to this. When you gain a Victory card, take the VP from this.

Setup: Put 8VP on the Silver and Gold piles.

## Official FAQ

- If you gain a card that is both a Treasure and a Victory card, such as Humble Castle, you can resolve the abilities in either order.

## Other rules clarifications

- Aqueduct only puts VP on Silver and Gold, but if you gain a different
- Treasure that has VP on its pile, you'll move 1VP to Aqueduct. For example:
- If you have Capitalism and then gain a Farmers' Market, you'll move 1VP from the pile onto Aqueduct.
- Action Supply piles can have VP on them due to Defiled Shrine. If you rotate to e.g. Sunken Treasure, then gaining it would move 1VP to Aqueduct.
- Aqueduct can move VP from non-Supply piles, for example if Farmers' Market is the pile gained with Ferryman and you have bought Capitalism.
- If the card you gain is both a Treasure and an Action (because of Capitalism, or because it's Crown), you move 1VP each to Aqueduct and Defiled Shrine. If there's only 1VP on the pile, you choose which Landmark to move it to.
- If you gain a Treasure from somewhere other than the Supply (e.g. gaining a Gold from the trash with Treasurer), you'll still move the VP tokens from that card's pile.
- If gaining a Victory card causes you to gain a Treasure (e.g. Hoard makes you gain a Gold), you can move the VP from the Treasure pile to Aqueduct, and then immediately take them.
