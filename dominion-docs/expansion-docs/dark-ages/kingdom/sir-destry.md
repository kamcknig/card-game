Sir Destry is one of the ten Knights cards from Dark Ages. Like the other
Knights, he is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. He
also provides a little bit of terminal draw, at +2 Cards, similar to a Moat.

## card text

+2 Cards

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them
costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this,
trash this.
