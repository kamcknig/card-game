Reap is an Event from Menagerie. It gains you a Gold and plays it automatically
at the start of your next turn.

## Official FAQ

- This means you will start your next turn with a Gold in play and +$3$3
  from it.
- You discard the Gold that turn as normal.
- If multiple things happen at the start of your turn, you can order them.
- This does not skip your Action phase.

## card text

Gain a Gold, setting it aside. At the start of your next turn, play it.
