Marquis is an Action card from Allies. It is a terminal draw card that will
double a small handsize, but leaves you with a maximum of 10 cards even if you
play it from a larger hand.

## Official FAQ

- Even if you were unable to draw the full amount, you still discard down to
  10 cards in hand afterwards.

## card text

+1 Buy

+1 Card per card in your hand. Discard down to 10 cards in hand.
