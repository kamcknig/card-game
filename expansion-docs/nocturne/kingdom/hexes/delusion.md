Delusion is a Hex from Nocturne. It is a turn-worsening attack that prevents you from buying Actions for a turn. It uses the State Deluded to track this. Its effect cannot stack with Envy's.

## Official FAQ

- Deluded/Envious is two-sided; take it with the Deluded side face up.

## card text

If you don't have Deluded or Envious, take Deluded.
