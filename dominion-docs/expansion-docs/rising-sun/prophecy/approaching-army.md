Approaching Army is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once activated, it gives you extra $$ for playing Attack cards; and it
makes sure there is an Attack card to receive this benefit by adding an additional Attack to the Supply at the start of
the game. Alongside Young Witch, Approaching Army is therefore one of two things that causes the Supply to consist of
more than 10 Kingdom card piles.

## Official FAQ

- The Attack card added in setup is in addition to the usual 10 Kingdom cards, even if those already included an Attack
  card. For split piles (from Allies and Empires), a pile is an Attack pile if the randomizer card for it is an Attack.
- The added pile is a regular Kingdom card pile, and can be gained from like other piles. This setup occurs at the start
  of the game, and so affects the game even if the Prophecy never happens.
- Once the Prophecy has happened, you get +$1 from each Attack card you play; for Duration Attacks, this applies only on
  the turn the Duration Attack was played.
- The +$1 is the last thing the Attack card does when it's played. You get the +$1 even if you didn't follow the
  instructions on the Attack card; for example if you used a Way (from Menagerie)

## card text

After you play an Attack card, +$1.

Setup: Add an Attack kingdom card pile to the Supply.
