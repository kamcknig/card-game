Plateau Shepherds is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to use $2 cards as a
source of alt-VP if you accumulate Favors.

## Official FAQ

- For example, if you have five Favors, two Estates, and a Moat, you can make
  three pairs, for 6VP.

## Other rules clarifications

- Cards must cost exactly $2 to count, which means that Apothecary can't be
  paired with a Favor.
- Most forms of cost reduction (e.g. Bridge) have no effect when scoring.
  However, Cheap cards still cost $1 less when scoring, which may matter for
  Plateau Shepherds, and Flourishing Trade remains in effect, which definitely
  matters.

## card text

When scoring, pair up your Favors with cards you have costing $2, for 2VP per
pair.
