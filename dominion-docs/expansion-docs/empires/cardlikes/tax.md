Tax is an Event from Empires. Simply by being in a game, it causes the first buy of any card to give D. In addition, Tax can be bought to add more D to a pile, functioning as a kind of pseudo-Attack.

## card text

Add 2D to a Supply pile.

Setup: Add 1D to each Supply pile. When a player gains a card in their Buy phase, they take the D from its pile.

## Unofficial FAQ (2022)

- Every Supply pile starts with 1D, including Kingdom cards and basic cards like Silver.
- The Event itself, when bought, adds 2D to a single pile, whether or not that pile has any D on it already.
- The D is taken by the next player to gain a card from that pile in their Buy phase; gaining a card when it's not their Buy phase leaves the D on the pile.

## Other rules clarifications

Tax got errata in 2022 to trigger when you gain a card in your Buy phase.

- This works if you gain a card by buying it, or if you gain a card in another way (such as Advance or Charm).
- If you gain a Black Market, you can play it with Innovation before taking the D from its pile.
- If you gain a card during another player's Buy phase (e.g. with Messenger), you won't take the D on its pile.
