Wayfarer is an Action card from Menagerie. It is a terminal draw card that can
optionally gain a Silver. It costs $6 by default, and resets to that cost at
the start of each turn. However, the costs of all Wayfarers change to copy the
cost of the last gained card. For example, if you do gain a Silver when playing
a Wayfarer, Wayfarers then cost $3.

## Official FAQ

- Normally Wayfarer costs $6.
- When anyone gains a card other than a Wayfarer, Wayfarer's cost changes to
  that card's cost for the rest of the turn (or until another card is gained).
  For example, if you play a Wayfarer and choose to gain a Silver, Wayfarer then
  costs $3, the same as Silver; if you play Witch, each other player gains a
  Curse, and then Wayfarer costs $0, the same as Curse.
- Cards that lower costs, like Bridge from Intrigue, only apply to the other
  card, not to Wayfarer too (though they apply to Wayfarer if no other cards
  have been gained yet). For example, if you play Bridge, Wayfarer costs $5;
  if you then buy a Silver, at that point Wayfarer costs $2, the same as
  Silver.
- Wayfarer can have a cost with PP (from Alchemy) or DD (from Empires) in it.
- Buying Animal Fair via trashing an Action card changes the cost of Wayfarer
  to $7, Animal Fair's cost, not to trashing an Action card.
- See the Unusual Costs section.

## Other Rules clarifications

- All Wayfarers change their costs to the last gained card, including ones in
  other players' hands.
- If the cost of the last card gained changes after it was gained, the cost
  of Wayfarer changes too. For example, if you gain a Fisherman when it
  costs $5, Wayfarer costs $5. But if you then reshuffle, emptying out your
  discard pile, that changes the cost of Fisherman to $2 and thus Wayfarer's
  cost becomes $2 as well.
- The cost of Wayfarer can change while resolving other cards. For example,
  if you play a Stonemason and trash a $6-cost Wayfarer, you first gain a
  cheaper card, and then you gain a card costing less than that card. So if you
  first gain a Duchy, you would then gain a card costing less than Wayfarer (
  which now costs $5).
- Once a card is gained, Wayfarer's cost-changing effect overrides other
  cost-changing effects. For example, if there's a Favor token (from Family of
  Inventors on Wayfarers, it costs $5. But if you gain a Silver, Wayfarer will
  cost $3 (and the Favor token has no effect).
- If you get a card through means other than gaining it (e.g. exiling it from
  the Supply with Camel Train) or exchanging it (e.g. with Trader), that won't
  affect Wayfarer's cost.

## card text

+3 Cards

You may gain a Silver.

This has the same cost as the last other card gained this turn, if any.
