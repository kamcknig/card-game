Puzzle Box is a Treasure-Loot card from Plunder. Like many other Loot cards, it provides +$3 and +Buy; its
distinguishing feature is that it also allows you to save an unused card from your hand for your next turn.

## Official FAQ

If you set aside a card, the Puzzle Box itself is still discarded normally that turn.
The set-aside card goes into your hand after drawing for the next turn.

## card text

$3

+1 Buy

You may set aside a card from your hand face down. Put it into your hand at end of turn.
