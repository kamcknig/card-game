Omen is a card type from Rising Sun. Omens are cards that allow you to remove Sun tokens from Prophecies; once all
tokens have been removed, the Prophecy's abilities take effect. The number of tokens to be removed varies depending on
the number of players: it's 5 tokens with 2 players, 8 with 3, 10 with 4, 12 with 5, and 13 with 6.

The Omens are all Action cards with abilities that tend to be highly desirable, including trashers, villages, a curser,
and sources of non-terminal draw and cantrip money; this encourages players to gain and use them, making the Prophecies
more likely to be activated.

For information about rules and preparation refer to the sections on the Prophecy page.
