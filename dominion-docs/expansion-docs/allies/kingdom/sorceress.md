Sorceress is an Action-Attack-Augur card from Allies. It is a cantrip cursing
attack that only triggers when you can successfully guess the top card of your
deck. It has similarities to Familiar (as a cantrip curser) as well as other
cards that make you guess at the top card of your deck, like Mystic and Wishing
Well.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Augurs: Herb Gatherer,
Acolyte, and Sibyl.

## Official FAQ

- Name a card; if the top card of your deck has that name, each other player
  gains a Curse.
- You put the card into your hand whether or not it had the name you chose.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you have no cards in your deck or discard pile, no-one gains a Curse.

## card text

+1 Action

Name a card. Reveal the top card of your deck and put it into your hand. If it's
the named card, each other player gains a Curse.
