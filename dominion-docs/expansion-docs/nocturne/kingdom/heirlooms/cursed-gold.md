Cursed Gold is a Treasure-Heirloom card from Nocturne, replacing one of your starting Coppers when Pooka is in the Kingdom. It can give you a lot of money early, but at the expense of having to gain a Curse; you have to decide whether the $3 is worth the penalty.

## Official FAQ

- You can choose not to play Cursed Gold, and thus not gain a Curse.

## card text

$3

Gain a Curse
