Cemetery is a Victory card from Nocturne. It's an on-gain trasher worth 2VP. It has an Heirloom, Haunted Mirror.

## Official FAQ

- In games using this, replace one of your starting Coppers with a Haunted
  Mirror.
- When you gain a Cemetery, trash from zero to four cards from your hand.

## card text

2VP

When you gain this, trash up to 4 cards from your hand.

Heirloom: Haunted Mirror
