Palace is a Landmark from Empires. It rewards players with VP for accumulating comparable amounts of the three basic Treasures — Copper, Silver, and Gold.

## card text

When scoring, 3VP per set you have of Copper - Silver - Gold.

## Official FAQ

- For example, if you had 7 Coppers, 5 Silvers, and 2 Golds, that would be two sets of Copper - Silver - Gold, for 6VP total.

## Other rules clarifications

- An equivalent way to describe the scoring for Palace is that you get 3VP per copy of whichever of the three Treasures you have least of.
