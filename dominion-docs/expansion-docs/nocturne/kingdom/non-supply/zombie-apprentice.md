Zombie Apprentice is an Action-Zombie card from Nocturne. It can trash an Action for non-terminal draw. There is only one copy of Zombie Apprentice, and it is added to the trash when Necromancer is in the game.

## Official FAQ

- If you trash an Action card from your hand, you draw three cards and get
  +1 Action.

## card text

You may trash an Action card from your hand for +3 Cards and +1 Action.
