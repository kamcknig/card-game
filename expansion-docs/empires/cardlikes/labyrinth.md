Labyrinth is a Landmark from Empires. It holds a finite number of VP tokens, which players take from when they gain more than one card in a turn.

## card text

When you gain a 2nd card in one of your turns, take 2VP from here.

Setup: Put 6VP here per player.

## Official FAQ

- This can only happen once per turn per player.
- For example if you gain 4 cards in the same turn, only the second one will come with 2VP.

## Other Rules clarifications

- During a turn with Possession, no players will get any VP from this.
