Goat is a Treasure-Heirloom card from Nocturne, replacing one of your starting Coppers when Pixie is in the Kingdom. It provides $1 and can trash a card from your hand. This allows for very quick trashing, since you start with one in your deck.

## Official FAQ

- Trashing a card is optional.

## card text

$1

You may trash a card from your hand.
