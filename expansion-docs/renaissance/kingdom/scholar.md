Scholar is an Action card from Renaissance. It is a terminal draw-to-x card that facilitates deck cycling.

## Official FAQ

- If drawing causes you to shuffle, you will shuffle in the discarded cards.

## Other rules clarifications

- You discard all cards in your hand at once, then activate any abilities
  triggered by the discard one at a time, and then draw seven cards. For instance, if your hand includes two Trails, you discard both of them, allowing you play the Trails for +1 card each, and then draw 7 more cards, for a total new hand of nine cards.

## card text

Discard your hand. +7 Cards.
