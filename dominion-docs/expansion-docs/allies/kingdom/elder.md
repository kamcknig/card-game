Elder is an Action-Townsfolk card from Allies. It increases the number of
choices you are able to make when a card prompts you to "choose" something.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Townsfolk, Town Crier,
Blacksmith, and Miller. Both Town Crier and Blacksmith let you choose from
multiple options, which means there will always be targets for Elder's ability
in the Kingdom with it.

## Official FAQ

- You can play an Action card with no "choose" ability; it will simply do
  what it normally does.
- If you play one with a "choose" ability, you may take an extra choice, but
  don't have to; for example, when playing Count (from Dark Ages), you could
  choose to only get one thing from the first "choose" ability, but two from the
  second.
- If you choose multiple things, you do those things in the order listed on
  the card; for example, if you use Elder on Blacksmith and choose "draw until
  you have 6 cards in hand" and "+1 Card and +1 Action," you first draw up to 6,
  then get +1 Card and +1 Action.
- If you use Elder on Courtier (from Intrigue), you get one extra choice, not
  one extra choice per type.
- Elder doesn't affect all choices, just ones that say "choose" and have a
  list of options; for example Workshop gives you a choice of what card to gain,
  but Elder playing Workshop doesn't do anything extra.

## Other rules clarifications

- Some cards may appear like they're giving you a choice (e.g. Weaver, Barge,
  and Jester), but because they don't say "choose", they aren't affected by
  Elder.
- If you use Elder on Scrap and trash a card costing $0$0, you get one choice.
  But if you use Elder on Scrap and trash nothing (because you have no cards in
  hand), you get no choices. [1]
- Elder does not make Catacombs more useful: If you choose both options, you
  put the 3 cards into your hand, discard them (from your hand), and draw 3
  different cards.
- Normally, Elder will have no effect on Duration cards that give you a
  choice next turn. For example, if you use Elder on Amulet, you get an
  additional choice this turn, but you won't get an additional choice on the
  following turn. And if you use Elder on Cabin Boy, nothing special happens.
  Elder won't stay in play with the Duration.
- However, if you play an Elder at the start of your turn (e.g. with Contract)
  and have it play a Quartermaster), you can choose both its options this turn (
  but not on future turns).
- If you use Elder on Stronghold, you can get both +$3$3 now and +3 Cards
  next turn. Elder won't stay in play with the Stronghold.
- If you use Elder on an Action that gives choices, and then you replay that
  Action (e.g. with Royal Carriage or Scepter), you won't get an additional
  choice on the second play. [2]
- If Elder plays a card with choices, and that card was played using Way of
  the Chameleon, you both get an extra choice from that card, and switch
  +Cards / +$$. [3]
- If you use Elder on a Reckless card that gives choices, you can get an
  extra choice during both iterations. [4]
- Even though the word "choose" is used, Elder has no effect on cards such as
  Advisor, Necromancer, Pillage, Swindler, or a second Elder.

## card text

+$2

You may play an Action card from your hand. When it gives you a choice of
abilities (with "choose") this turn, you may choose an extra (different) option.
