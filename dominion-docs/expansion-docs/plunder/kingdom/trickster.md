Trickster is an Action-Attack card from Plunder. It is a curser that also allows you to save a Treasure you play and put
it back in your hand for your next turn.

## Official FAQ

This is cumulative; if you play two Tricksters, then you can set aside up to two Treasures you discard from play and put
them into your hand at end of turn, after drawing.

## card text

Each other player gains a Curse. Once this turn, when you discard a Treasure from play, you may set it aside. Put it in
your hand at end of turn.
