Kind Emperor is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it is activated, for the rest of the game, you gain a free Action of
your choice directly into your hand at the beginning of each turn.

## Official FAQ

- You gain any Action to your hand, regardless of cost. This is not optional.
- When the last Sun is removed, this applies immediately, in the middle of resolving the Omen, and only the player who
  removed the Sun gains an Action then.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you remove the last Sun at the start of your turn (e.g. you play a Poet with Delay), you'll gain 2 Actions to your
  hand.

## card text

At the start of your turn, and when you remove the last Sun: Gain an Action to your hand.
