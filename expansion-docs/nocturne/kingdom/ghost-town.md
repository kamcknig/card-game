Ghost Town is a Night-Duration card from Nocturne. It's a delayed village, doing nothing when it's played, but drawing a card and giving an Action on your next turn. It's also conveniently gained to your hand, so it can be played immediately.

## Official FAQ

- Since Night is after the Buy phase, normally you can play this the turn
  you buy it.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you gain this onto your deck (with e.g. Armory), you didn't gain it to
  your discard pile, so Ghost Town's ability doesn't trigger and it stays on your deck.

## card text

At the start of your next turn, +1 Card and +1 Action.

This is gained to your hand (instead of your discard pile).
