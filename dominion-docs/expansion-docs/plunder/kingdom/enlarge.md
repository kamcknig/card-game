Enlarge is an Action-Duration card from Plunder. It is a remodeler that remodels
two cards: one when you play it, and one at the beginning of your next turn. It
allows for quick improvement of your deck, but it can be risky, since it makes
you commit to trashing a card before you know which cards you'll be choosing
from.

## Official FAQ

Once you've played Enlarge, trashing a card at the start of your next turn is
mandatory.

## card text

Now and at the start of your next turn: Trash a card from your hand, and gain
one costing up to $2 more.
