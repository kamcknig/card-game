League of Shopkeepers is an Ally from Allies. It adds additional vanilla bonuses
to your Liaison cards once you have accumulated enough Favor tokens.

## Official FAQ

- You don't spend Favors to use this.
- After each time you play a Liaison, you get +$1$1 if you have 5 or more
  Favors, and +1 Action and +1 Buy (in addition to the +$1$1) if you have 10 or
  more.
- In games with multiple Liaisons, all of the Liaisons get the bonus, even if
  only one of them was used to get the Favors.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you have 3 Favors, and you use Throne Room on Underling, you only get
  +$1$1 from League of Shopkeepers.
- However, if you have 3 Favors, play an Underling, and then replay it with
  either Royal Carriage or Citadel, League of Shopkeepers will trigger twice,
  and you can get +$2$2 from it.
- If you have 10+ Favors and play either a Bauble or a Contract, you can get
  +1 Action in your Buy phase; this won't let you play more Actions from your
  hand.
- If you gain a Sycophant, get +2 Favors, and then play it with Innovation,
  the 2 Favors will count for League of Shopkeepers.
- However, if you gain a Guildmaster and play it with Innovation, the
  Guildmaster will give you +1 Favor from gaining itself. However, you won't get
  that Favor in time for League of Shopkeepers.

## card text

After playing a Liaison, if you have 5 or more Favors, +$1, and if 10 or more,
+1 Action and +1 Buy.
