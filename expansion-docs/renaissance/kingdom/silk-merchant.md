Silk Merchant is an Action card from Renaissance. It provides terminal draw and +Buy, as well Coffers and Villagers when it is gained or trashed.

## Official FAQ

- When you play this, you get +2 Cards and +1 Buy; when you trash it or gain
  it, you get +1 Coffers and +1 Villager.
- If Silk Merchant is trashed, the player trashing it takes the +1 Coffers
  and +1 Villager, regardless of whose turn it is.

## card text

+2 Cards

+1 Buy

When you gain or trash this, +1 Coffers and +1 Villager.
