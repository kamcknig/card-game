Fisherman is an Action card from Menagerie. It is a Peddler variant whose cost
drops to $2 when your discard pile is empty.

## Official FAQ

- This costs $5 normally, but $2 whenever the player whose turn it is has an
  empty discard pile.
- If you have $4 and two Buys with an empty discard pile, you can buy
  Fisherman for $2, but then you will no longer have an empty discard pile, so
  you cannot buy a second for $2.
- See the Unusual Costs section.

## card text

+1 Card

+1 Action

+$1

During your turns, if your discard pile is empty, this costs $3 less.
