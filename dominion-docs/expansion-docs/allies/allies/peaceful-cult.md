The Peaceful Cult is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to use Favor tokens to
trash cards from your hand.

## Official FAQ

- Spend the Favors all at once; then choose all the cards to trash; then
  trash them; then resolve things that happen due to trashing those cards, in
  any order.

## Other rules clarifications

- If trashing those cards gets you more Favors (e.g. by trashing a Sycophant)
  or more cards in your hand (e.g. by trashing a Cultist), you won't be able to
  trash more cards with Peaceful Cult.
- If you take multiple Buy phases in a turn (with e.g. Villa or Cavalry), you
  can trash with Peaceful Cult multiple times a turn.

## card text

At the start of your Buy phase, you may spend any number of Favors to trash that
many cards from your hand.
