Den of Sin is a Night-Duration card from Nocturne. It provides a larger handsize on your next turn, and is gained to hand for immediate use.

## Official FAQ

- Since Night is after the Buy phase, normally you can play this the turn
  you buy it.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you gain this onto your deck (with e.g. Armory), you didn't gain it to
  your discard pile, so Den of Sin's ability doesn't trigger and it stays on your deck.

## card text

At the start of your next turn, +2 Cards.

This is gained to your hand (instead of your discard pile).
