Modify is an Action card from Allies. It can serve as either a cantrip trasher
or a remodeler.

## Official FAQ

- First trash a card from your hand. Then, choose whether to take +1 Card and
  +1 Action, or to gain a card costing up to $2 more than the trashed card.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you play this with Elder and pick both choices, you do them in the
  printed order, meaning you draw a card before gaining a card. This means you
  can't draw the card that you just gained. But if the card you draw is a
  Reaction that cares about gaining cards (such as Sheepdog), you can use it.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand. Choose one: +1 Card and +1 Action; or gain a card
costing up to $2 more than the trashed card.
