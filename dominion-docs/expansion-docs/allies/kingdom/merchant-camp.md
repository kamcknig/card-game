Merchant Camp is an Action card from Allies. It is a non-drawing village that
can top-deck itself at the end of your turn—so you can keep drawing it every
turn if you want to, getting a reliable source of extra Action plays at the cost
of slowing down your cycling.

## Official FAQ

- If you have multiple Merchant Camps in play, you can choose how many you
  want to put on top of your deck.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you play this using a Way, or under the influence of Enchantress, you
  can still top-deck this when you discard it from play.

## card text

+2 Actions

+$1

When you discard this from play, you may put it onto your deck.
