Citadel is a Project from Renaissance. Each turn it lets you play your first Action card twice, as if you had first played a Throne Room. Citadel only lets you play the Action card for free the second time, the first time still uses your Action play.

## Official FAQ

- Once you've claimed this ability, it is not optional.
- This can affect an Action card played outside of the Action phase, if it
  is your first Action card played that turn; for example if you also had Capitalism, you could opt to play a Flag Bearer in your Buy phase as your first play of the turn, and it would still be played twice.
- Citadel can cause a Duration card to be played twice; you will have to
  remember that on your next turn.

## Other rules clarifications

- Like most other Throne Room variants, Citadel will still replay the Action
  card even if it is no longer in the play area after the first time it gets played (e.g., if it is a one-shot such as Acting Troupe. If this happens, the Action card is not moved back to the play area but its instructions are followed a second time.
- If the first Action card you play is affected by Enchantress, you get +1
  Card and +1 Action, then Citadel replays the card for its normal ability.
- You can use Citadel's ability on the turn you buy it if you play your
  first Action card after buying Citadel.
- If you play a card, but then play a second card before you follow the
  first card's instructions, Citadel still replays the first card.

## card text

The first time you play an Action card during each of your turns, replay it afterwards.
