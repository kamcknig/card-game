Annex is an Event from Empires. It allows the buyer to gain a Duchy and reshuffle immediately, leaving out up to five cards that they don't want to draw before their next reshuffle.

## card text

Look through your discard pile. Shuffle all but up to 5 cards from it into your deck. Gain a Duchy.

## Official FAQ (2021)

- You can do this even if the Duchy pile is empty.
- The chosen cards stay in your discard pile when the other cards are shuffled into your deck.
- If you shuffle zero cards into your deck, you still shuffle.
