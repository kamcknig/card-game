Figurehead is a Treasure-Duration-Loot card from Plunder. Like most other Loot cards, it provides +$3; but instead of
giving +Buy, it is also a source of duration draw.

## Official FAQ

When you play this, you get +$3 and at the start of your next turn, you get +2 Cards.

## card text

$3

At the start of your next turn, +2 Cards.
