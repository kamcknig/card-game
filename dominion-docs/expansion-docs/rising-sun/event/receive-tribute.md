Receive Tribute is an Event from Rising Sun. It can allow you to gain three cards at a time, under a somewhat
restrictive set of circumstances: you can only gain Action cards you don't have copies of in play, and it only works if
you've already gained three or more cards before buying the event.

## Official FAQ

The Action cards you gain need to all have different names from each other and from cards you have in play.
You gain them one at a time, in any order.
You don't have to gain the full three.

## Other rules clarifications

Actions with DD or PP in their cost can be gained. You do not take any DD for this or need to have any Potion in play.

## card text

If you've gained at least 3 cards this turn, gain up to 3 differently named Action cards you don't have copies of in
play.
