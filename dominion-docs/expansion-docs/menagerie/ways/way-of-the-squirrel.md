Way of the Squirrel is a Way from Menagerie. It allows you to start your next
turn with 2 additional cards.

## Official FAQ

- Normally you get the two cards after drawing your hand in Clean-up. If you
  use this when it is not your turn (such as via Black Cat), you still draw two
  cards at the end of the turn.

## card text

+2 Cards at the end of this turn.
