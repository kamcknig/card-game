Sacrifice is an Action card from Empires. It is a trasher that gives a variable bonus depending on the type of the card it trashed.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand. If it's an…

Action card, +2 Cards and +2 Actions

Treasure card, +$2

Victory card, +2VP

## Official FAQ

- If you trash a card with multiple types, you get all relevant bonuses; for example if you trash Crown, you get +2 Cards, +2 Actions, and +$2.
- If you trash a card with no relevant types (such as Curse), you get nothing.

## Other rules clarifications

- Sacrificing an Estate under the influence of Inheritance will give +2 Cards, +2 Actions, and +2 VP.
