Canal is a Project from Renaissance. It provides permanent cost reduction on your turns only.

## Official FAQ

- During your turns, all cards, including cards in the Supply, in hands, and
  in Decks, cost \$1 less, but not less than $0.
- For example if you have Canal and play Villain, other players discard a
  card costing at least \$2, which could not be Estate, as Estate only costs
  $1 on your turns.

## Other rules Clarifications

- At the end of the game, it is no longer your turn, so Canal does not
  affect scoring for Plateau Shepherds.

## card text

During your turns, cards cost $1 less.
