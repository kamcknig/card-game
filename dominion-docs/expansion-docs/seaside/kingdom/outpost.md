Outpost is an Action-Duration card from Seaside. When played, it lets you take an extra turn — albeit an extra turn where you draw fewer cards.

## Official FAQ

- The extra turn is normal except for the smaller starting hand.
- If for example you play Merchant Ship and Outpost on the same turn, then
  the extra turn will be your next turn, and you'll get +$2 from Merchant Ship then.
- Outpost always causes you to only draw 3 cards for your next hand, but
  only gives you an extra turn if, when the time comes for that turn, you have not taken the previous two turns.
- So if you play two Outposts on the same turn, you will only get one extra
  turn; if you play an Outpost during an extra turn, it will give you a smaller hand but won't give you an extra turn.
- Extra turns do not count towards the tiebreaker of which tied player had
  fewer turns.

## Other rules clarifications

- You don't get to take any extra turns after the game has ended.
- With Fleet, you can take extra turns after the normal game end, but not
  after the final Fleet turn.
- You only draw 3 cards, even if you know you won't get the extra turn, and
  even if the Outpost turn won't be the next turn.
- If you play Outpost multiple times with a card like Throne Room, Throne
  Room stays in play with Outpost even though you won't get a second extra turn.
- You can't be sure Outpost will fail until you are in between turns,
  determining what turn happens next. If Outpost fails, or its turn is skipped by Lich, you discard it during Clean-up of the next turn, whoever's turn it is.
- For example, if you play Outpost and Voyage, then in Clean-up you don't
  discard either of them and only draw 3 cards. In between turns, you choose to take the Voyage turn next. (Outpost hasn't failed yet because another player might somehow be able to take an extra turn first.) In Clean-up of the Voyage turn, you discard Voyage but not Outpost and draw 5 cards. In between turns again, Outpost now fails - it is up next but would be your 3rd turn in a row. The next player takes their turn and during their Clean-up, you discard Outpost.
- If you play Outpost on another player's turn, you draw 3 cards in your
  next Clean-up, which is usually during the Outpost turn.
- If multiple players play Outpost on the same turn, the extra turns go in
  turn order, starting with the player who took the most recent regular turn.
- You can take 2 Outpost turns in a row if the preceding turn wasn't yours.

## card text

You only draw 3 cards for your next hand. Take an extra turn after this one (but not a 3rd turn in a row).
