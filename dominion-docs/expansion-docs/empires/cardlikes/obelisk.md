Obelisk is a Landmark from Empires. It rewards players with 2VP for each card they have from an Action Supply pile randomly chosen at the start of the game.

## card text

When scoring, 2VP per card you have from the chosen pile.

Setup: Choose a random Action Supply pile.

## Official FAQ

- All cards from the chosen pile count, even if they have different names (such as when it is a split pile).
- Ruins (from Dominion: Dark Ages), when used, can be the pile.

## Additional rules clarifications

- If the chosen Action card is also a Victory card, they will be worth an additional 2VP. This means that Great Hall and Mill are worth 3VP, while Dame Josephine, Island and Nobles are now worth 4VP.
