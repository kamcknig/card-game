Desperation is an Event from Menagerie. It allows the player to add $2$2 to
their total at the cost of gaining a Curse. It is similar to Borrow or Cursed
Gold.

## Official FAQ

- If the Curse pile is empty, you fail to gain one and do not get +1 Buy and
  +$2$2.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you gain the Curse but then remove it from your deck (e.g. you trash it
  with Watchtower, or reveal a Trader and exchange it), you'll get +1 Buy
  and +$2$2. However, if you never gain the Curse (e.g. you buy Desperation
  during a Possession turn), you won't get +1 Buy and +$2$2.

## card text

Once per turn: You may gain a Curse. If you do, +1 Buy and +$2$2.
