Good Harvest is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once it is activated, you get an extra +$1 and +Buy for every differently
named Treasure you play on a turn.

## Official FAQ

- For example if you play 4 Coppers and a Silver, you'd get +2 Buys and +$2 total from Good Harvest.
- If you played a Treasure in the same turn before removing the last Sun token from Good Harvest, it doesn't
  retroactively give you +1 Buy and +$1.

## card text

The first time you play each differently named Treasure each turn, first, +1 Buy and +$1.
