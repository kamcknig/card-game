Bauble is a Treasure-Liaison card from Allies. Like Pawn, it allows you to
choose two from a range of minor effects; it can be used as a source of +Buy or
Favors, or to top-deck gained cards.

## Official FAQ

- Choose two of the four options; the first three options are simple +1's,
  and the last is everything else. So for example you could choose to take +1
  Buy and "this turn, when you gain a card, you may put it onto your deck."

## Other rules clarifications

- Choosing the top-decking option on Bauble once is sufficient to top-deck
  any number of gained cards for the rest of the turn. If you play two Baubles
  and choose top-decking for both of them, the second top-decking choice confers
  no additional benefit.

## card text

Choose two different options: +1 Buy; +$1; +1 Favor; this turn, when you gain a
card, you may put it onto your deck.
