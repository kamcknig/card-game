Town Crier is an Action-Townsfolk card from Allies. It gives you a choice
between using it as a terminal silver, a cantrip, or a Silver gainer.

It is part of a split pile that it shares with the other Townsfolk: Blacksmith,
Miller, and Elder; and it allows rotating the split pile.

## Official FAQ

- First choose either to get +$2:, or to gain a Silver, or to get +1 Card
  and +1 Action. Then, no matter what you picked, choose whether or not to
  rotate the Townsfolk pile.

## card text

Choose one: +$2; or gain a Silver; or +1 Card and +1 Action.

You may rotate the Townsfolk.
