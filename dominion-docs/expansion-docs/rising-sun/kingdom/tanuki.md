Tanuki is an Action-Shadow card from Rising Sun. It is a remodeler that can be played from your deck as well as from
your hand.

## Official FAQ

- See the Shadows section.
- When you play this, you trash a card from your hand, and gain a card costing up to $2$2 more than it, like when
  playing Remodel.

## card text

Trash a card from your hand. Gain a card costing up to $2 more than it.

You can play this from your deck as if in your hand.
