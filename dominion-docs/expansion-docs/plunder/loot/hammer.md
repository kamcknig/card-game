Hammer is a Treasure-Loot card from Plunder. Like most other Loot cards, it provides +$3; but instead of giving +Buy, it
acts as a Workshop variant instead.

## Official FAQ

Each time you play this, you get +$3 and gain a card costing up to $4. This isn't optional.

## card text

$3

Gain a card costing up to $4.
