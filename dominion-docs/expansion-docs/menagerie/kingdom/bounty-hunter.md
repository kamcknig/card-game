Bounty Hunter is an Action card from Menagerie. It non-terminally Exiles a card
from your hand, and gives +$3 if you didn't have a copy of it on your Exile mat
already.

## Official FAQ

- First you get +1 Action and Exile a card from your hand; this is not optional.
- Then if that is the only copy of that card that you have in Exile, you get
  +$3.
- If you can't Exile a card, you don't get +$3.

## card text

+1 Action

Exile a card from your hand. If you didn't have a copy of it in Exile, +$3.
