The League of Bankers is an Ally from Allies. It rewards you with virtual coin
for accumulating Favors.

## Official FAQ

- You don't spend Favors to use this; you just get +$$ based on the number of
  Favors you've accumulated.

## Other rules clarifications

- If you gain extra Favors at the start of your Buy phase (e.g. you discard a
  Weaver to Arena, and then gain a Sycophant), that may give you more +$$ from
  this.
- If you take multiple Buy phases in a turn (with e.g. Villa or Cavalry), you
  can get +$$ from this multiple times.

## card text

At the start of your Buy phase, +$1 per 4 Favors you have (round down).
