Staff is a Treasure-Loot card from Plunder. Like many other Loot cards, it provides +$3 and +Buy; its distinguishing
feature is that it also allows you to play an Action card during your Buy phase, making it in effect a type of village.

## Official FAQ

Playing an Action card from your hand is optional.

## card text

$3

+1 Buy

You may play an Action from your hand.
