Fellowship of Scribes is an Ally from Allies. It allows you to spend Favor
tokens to draw an extra card if your handsize is small, but you have to play an
Action card first.

## Official FAQ

- You can only do this once per time you play an Action card.
- Completely resolve the Action card; then if you have 4 or fewer cards in
  hand, you may spend a Favor for +1 Card.

## card text

After playing an Action, if you have 4 or fewer cards in hand, you may spend a
Favor for +1 Card.
