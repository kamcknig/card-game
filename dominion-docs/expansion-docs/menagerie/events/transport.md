Transport is an Event from Menagerie. It can put an Action card from the Supply
onto your Exile mat or take an Action from your Exile mat onto your deck,
letting you get any Action card you want with two purchases. It is the only way
to retrieve uniquely-named cards, such as Knights, from the Exile mat, as there
is no way to gain another copy of them.

## Official FAQ

- It only matters if the card is an Action, not if the whole pile is.
- This can take Action cards from your Exile mat that were put there by other
  cards.

## card text

Choose one: Exile an Action card from the Supply; or put an Action card you have
in Exile onto your deck.
