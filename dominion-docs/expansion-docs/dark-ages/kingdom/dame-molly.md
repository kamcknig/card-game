Dame Molly is one of the ten Knights from Dark Ages. Like the other Knights, she is an Action-Attack-Knight that trashes other players' cards. She is also a village since she gives +2 Actions.

## card text

+2 Actions

Each other player reveals the top 2 cards of their deck, trashes one of them costing from $3 to $6, and discards the rest. If a Knight is trashed by this, trash this.
