Sprawling Castle is a Victory-Castle card from Empires. It is the sixth of eight different Castles that make up the Castles split pile and comes with one or more additional Victory cards when gained.

## card text

4VP

When you gain this, gain a Duchy or 3 Estates.

## Official FAQ

- You can pick either option on Sprawling Castle regardless of how many Duchies and Estates are left in the piles.
