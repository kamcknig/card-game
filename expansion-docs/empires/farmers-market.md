Farmers' Market is an Action-Gathering card from Empires. It is a +Buy card that Gathers  VP tokens and gives +$ for the first four out of five times played, whether by you or another player; it then trashes itself on the fifth play, and collects the Gathered VP. It is thus a one-shot if played when there are 4VP on its Supply pile.

## card text

+1 Buy

If there are 4VP or more on the Farmers' Market pile, take them and trash this. Otherwise, add 1VP to the pile and then +$1 per 1VP on the pile.

## Official FAQ

- The first time this is played, it produces +\$1 (and +1 Buy), the next time +\$2, then +\$3, then +\$4, then the next time the player takes the 4VP (and gets no +\$), then the next time it is back to +$1.
- This still functions if the Farmers' Market pile is empty.

## Other Rules Clarifications

- If you gain this from Black Market, or when it's set aside with Way of the Mouse, there is no Farmers' Market pile. This means no  VP tokens will accumulate, and you won't get any +$; the card does nothing but +1 Buy.
- If you play this with a Command variant, the Farmers' Market will fail to trash itself, but you still take 4 VP.
