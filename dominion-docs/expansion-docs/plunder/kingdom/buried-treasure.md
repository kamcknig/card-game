Buried Treasure is a Treasure-Duration card from Plunder. It provides +Buy and +$3$3 on the turn after you play it—including the turn after you gain it as well, since it plays itself automatically on gain.

## Official FAQ
When you gain this, you have to play it; it's not optional.

## card text

At the start of your next turn, +1 Buy and +$3.

When you gain this, play it.
