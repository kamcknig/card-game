Great Leader is a Prophecy from Rising Sun. Once activated, it gives everyone unlimited terminal space for the rest of
the game.

## Official FAQ

Since every Action card you play gives you at least +1 Action, you'll always be able to play all of your Action cards,
barring explicit exceptions like Snowy Village (from Menagerie).

## Other rules clarifications

As Prophecies activate immediately upon removing the last Sun token, the Omen that removed the last token will receive
+1 Action.

## card text

After each Action card you play, +1 Action.
