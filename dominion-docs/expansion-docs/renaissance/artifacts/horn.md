The Horn is an Artifact from Renaissance. It lets you top-deck one Border Guard each turn when discarding it from play. This makes it easier to repeatedly try to reveal only Actions to take the Lantern. There is one copy of it, and players may be able to take it from each other when playing a Border Guard.

see [horn](../kingdom/border-guard.md) for faq

## card text

Once per turn, when you discard a Border Guard from play, you may put it onto your deck.
